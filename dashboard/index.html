Where the candidates are

Watchlist edges by funnel stage. Gate 0 is the archive; gate 1 is days nobody had seen when the rule was frozen.

Paper net to date, by lane

Daily books: summed native on-time net on dates present for every listed book, averaged across books. Replay and late rows remain separate. Event lanes: mean per scored ticket.

Candidate follow-ups

Selected follow-ups, not a second methods queue or proof of an edge. Evidence may be exploratory or holdout; read the evidence column and the linked round before comparing t values.

Every event strategy tested: size against strength

One dot per strategy: mean net return per event at 1x cost (symmetric log scale) against its t. Rings indicate an explicit pass in the source report, not approval for live trading. t = 2 alone does not account for the research search.

Strategy table

Per-event stats from family reports (qx) and event-lane verdicts. RC p is the family reality check; null p the matched-date null.

Cumulative paper net, daily books

Sum of daily net returns at 1x cost, not compounded. Replay and on-time rows retain their own labels. A missing return makes the subsequent cumulative total unknown.

Forward lanes

Progress counts only what decides the lane: on-time days for daily books, scored tickets or notices for event lanes.

Event tickets

Unlock, delisting and Monitoring Tag tickets, frozen before entry, plus the unlocks due next on the recorded calendar.

Tear sheet

Compounded daily net at 1x cost. Forward paper series are weeks old; backtests are gate 0 (in-sample or already-spent windows). Sharpe is shown naive and adjusted for autocorrelation (Lo 2002). PSR and the deflated Sharpe use the effective sample size, and the deflated Sharpe is taken at the program's full trial count, so it is a deliberately harsh bar.

Monthly returns

Compounded net per calendar month for the selected series; the last column is the year.

All series

One row per paper book or saved research stream. NW t is the Newey-West t of the daily mean; n eff is the AR(1) effective number of independent days.

Best result per round against what chance gives

Filled dot: the round's best t from its family report. Hollow dot: the largest t written in its registry note (parsed text, approximate). Dash: the best t that pure chance reaches among that many independent tries. Diamond: the family's own reality-check null (95th percentile of its max t), where one was run; values off the top are clipped and labelled. A best t below either bar is what the search alone would produce.

Program trials, cumulative

Every strategy tried, all rounds, log scale. Each new result is read against this total, not only against its own family.

Cumulative rank IC by factor

Each line adds up the daily Spearman correlation between a factor and the next return across coins (approximated as each month's mean times its days). A line that climbs steadily is a factor that ranks coins the same way month after month; a line that wanders around zero is noise. Backtest on the archive (gate 0). Whether a factor passes is decided by its frozen reality check, not by this chart.

Widest cross-venue funding spreads

Latest hourly snapshot of seven venues (MEXC, Gate, Hyperliquid, OKX, Bitget, KuCoin, dYdX), each rate scaled to 8 hours; spread = highest minus lowest venue, annualized at 3 settlements a day. Descriptive only: the same ticker on two venues is not proven to be the same coin, and a spread pays only if it persists after price moves and costs, which a frozen lane must test. Method queue

The canonical methods registry. Untested and partial rules remain candidates; blocked rows retain the missing input or mechanics.

Operations by responsibility

Collection, paper scoring, decisions and reporting have distinct categories. A recent legacy log is unverified activity, not a successful run. Structured success means exit zero only; observation quality is assessed separately. Process command lines and raw log contents are not exported.

Data coverage

First to last bar of each downloaded dataset (from its provenance record) and first to last day of each live recorder. Holes are missing hours between a file's first and last bar, summed over files.

Data sources

Folder metadata grouped by purpose. File activity does not prove recent market observations or complete history. Partial scans exclude unreadable entries and symbolic links; provenance means a note exists, not that its contents were verified.
