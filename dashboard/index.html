Where the candidates are

Watchlist edges by funnel stage. Gate 0 is the archive; gate 1 is days nobody had seen when the rule was frozen.

Paper net to date, by lane

Daily books: summed daily net, mean of the lane's books (replay and on-time). Event lanes: mean per scored ticket.

Candidate follow-ups

Selected follow-ups, not a second methods queue or proof of an edge. Evidence may be exploratory or holdout; read the evidence column and the linked round before comparing t values.

Every event strategy tested: size against strength

One dot per strategy: mean net return per event at 1x cost (symmetric log scale) against its t. Rings indicate an explicit pass in the source report, not approval for live trading. t = 2 alone does not account for the research search.

Strategy table

Per-event stats from family reports (qx) and event-lane verdicts. RC p is the family reality check; null p the matched-date null.

Cumulative paper net, daily books

Sum of daily net returns at 1x cost, not compounded. Replay and on-time rows retain their own labels. A missing return makes the subsequent cumulative total unknown.

Forward lanes

Progress counts only what decides the lane: on-time days for daily books, scored tickets or notices for event lanes.

Event tickets

Unlock, delisting and Monitoring Tag tickets, frozen before entry, plus the unlocks due next on the recorded calendar.

Rounds by verdict

Every registered round. A null is a result.

Registry

All rounds in registry order, with their trial counts.

Methods by status

The queue of methods from the literature, old sessions and both AIs.

Method queue

The canonical methods registry. Untested and partial rules remain candidates; blocked rows retain the missing input or mechanics.

Operations by responsibility

Collection, paper scoring, decisions and reporting have distinct categories. A recent legacy log is unverified activity, not a successful run. Structured success means exit zero only; observation quality is assessed separately. Process command lines and raw log contents are not exported.

Data sources

Each folder under data/: size, newest file and whether a provenance note exists. Live recorders are stale after two hours.
