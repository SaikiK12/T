# Edge map: every way the lab could make money, and what each one has shown

Generated by `scripts/edge_map.py` from `registry/edge_families.csv`, `registry/edge_map_links.csv`,
`registry/methods.csv` and `registry/rounds.csv`. Do not edit by hand; edit the registry files and regenerate.
The live queue stays `registry/methods.csv`; this file only places each method and round in one family.

## The master equation
Every strategy the lab can run, on any venue, nets

```
net = sum over trades [ d x dP/P            price move in the position's direction
                      + Phi                 funding and settlements received
                      + R ]                 rebates, incentives, collateral yield
    - sum over trades [ fee + spread + impact(size) + latency slippage ]
    - financing - tail losses (liquidations, delistings, gaps)
```

An edge is a term that is positive in expectation *after* the cost line, for a reason that should persist (someone
pays it), and that survives selection: its t-statistic is read against `registry.program_trials()`, not its own null.
Each family below names the term it lives in, who pays it, and the constraint that has decided its verdicts so far.

## Fake-edge checklist (apply to every result, positive or null)
1. **Cost:** a band or average cost instead of each coin's book at the minute of the trade (audit F1, F7); the web
   fee for API fills (F3, F8); maker fills assumed at the touch.
2. **Prices:** stale or delisted prices (F4, F5); last trade vs mark; fills at a bar close that was not tradable.
3. **Time:** data used before it was published; DST and session windows (D1); late-computed hours (F9); joining
   different windows (F10); a payment credited before the exit price that earns it (F2).
4. **Accounting:** constant-notional vs fixed-quantity marks (H2); funding-interval scaling (H3, R384);
   day-weighted vs trade-weighted means (H4); cluster vs leg weighting (H5).
5. **Universe:** survivorship; contract name mis-joins (H6); a freshness rule that depends on bar length (H1);
   reserved or already-read rows.
6. **Selection:** best of many without the program's trial count; post hoc picks; a sum carried by a few days
   (top-10-day share); leave-two-coins-out and trimmed means.
7. **Statistics:** test size (F11); clustering that does nothing (D3); missing trial counts (D4); short samples.
8. **Execution:** capacity on thin coins; margin and liquidation per venue; venue access.

The audit ids refer to `docs/reviews/hidden_edge_audit_20261104.md`. A null can be fake too: H1 hid dYdX's carry.

## P1 Cross-venue funding carry (Risk premia)

- **Who pays:** Leveraged traders on the venue whose funding is higher; capital and margin cannot move freely between venues.
- **Equation:** `day net = sum_pairs[(f_short - f_long) + (r_long - r_short)] / 2K - c x legs traded / 2K; pays when E[f_short - f_long] over the hold > 4c`
- **Binding constraint:** Cost and capacity on thin coins; per-venue margin; delisting and freshness accounting.
- **Fake-edge checks:** Freshness rule by bar length (audit H1); constant-notional marks (H2); stale delisted legs (F4, F5); band vs per-coin cost (F7); funding-interval scaling (R384); name mis-joins (H6).
- **Methods (8):** BLOCKED 1, PARTIAL 2, QUEUED 3, TESTED 1, UNTESTED 1. M059, M241, M409, M410, M449, M468, M469, M470
- **Rounds:** confirmed R288, R391; partial R275, R277, R312, R313, R314, R315, R316, R317, R318, R319, R322, R323, R384, R401, R402; null none; running R278, R279, R294, R390, R404; pending R310, R311
- **Reading:** The only family confirmed on data nobody had read (R288, R391), and it is small: R391 earns +2.5%/yr on book capital. No forward lane has decided yet. The audit found artifacts in both directions: H1 hides dYdX, F4/F5 inflate R278.
- **Gaps:** Venue-neutral engine; dYdX long tail (H1); measured thin-coin costs (R401/R402 priced 2 of 28 contracts); collateral yield on top; capacity per coin.
- **Next:** M468 engine, then M469 re-test at measured costs; M470 dead-leg split of R278-R390.

## P2 Spot-perp carry and dated basis (Risk premia)

- **Who pays:** Leveraged longs who pay funding or a futures premium to hold exposure.
- **Equation:** `net = sum f_t - (basis_exit - basis_entry) - 4c - borrow; dated: (F - S)/S at entry - 4c, held to expiry`
- **Binding constraint:** Funding level is regime-bound; spot and perp fees on both legs.
- **Fake-edge checks:** Funding-interval changes; spot borrow availability; survivorship of coins with spot markets.
- **Methods (3):** BLOCKED 1, PARTIAL 1, TESTED 1. M047, M134, M244
- **Rounds:** confirmed R185, R190, R191; partial R324, R325, R326, R327, R328; null none; running R220; data_pass R335
- **Reading:** Real but regime-bound: R190/R191 earned about +35% in 2021, ~0 in 2022-23, +8% in 2024 and about 1%/yr since. Dated cash-and-carry rounds R324-R328 are PARTIAL.
- **Gaps:** Regime trigger for when the premium comes back; dated futures on more venues.
- **Next:** No new test; keep a regime monitor and combine with P1 capital.

## P4 Liquidity provision and market making (Risk premia)

- **Who pays:** Impatient takers pay the spread; makers bear adverse selection and inventory risk.
- **Equation:** `per fill: spread/2 + rebate - E[adverse move after fill | fill] - inventory cost; pays when spread/2 + rebate > adverse selection`
- **Binding constraint:** Queue position and fill realism; needs live quoting, which only the owner can run.
- **Fake-edge checks:** Optimistic fill model (touch vs trade-through); ignoring queue; paper fills that a real resting order would not get; adverse selection measured after the fact.
