# Cross-lane scan, 2026-11-17: where the edge and the losses sit in what the lab already ran

The owner asked (2026-11-17) to search wide, across all the old lanes, and then refine; and for data to analyze.
This page is the wide pass over what is already stored. It is evidence for **choosing** what to refine. It is not a
test.

- **Tool.** `scripts/lane_scan.py` (tested in `tests/test_lane_scan.py`) reads two kinds of results and puts them in one
  table:
  - every committed summary table with a daily net (`mean_bp_day`);
  - every report JSON holding an annualized net and a t.
- **Output.**
  - [`lane_scan_20261117/cells.csv`](lane_scan_20261117/cells.csv): one row per lane, table, label and window, with
    net, gross, cost, break-even cost multiple, t, days, Sharpe, drawdown and the lane's registry rounds and verdicts;
  - [`summary.json`](lane_scan_20261117/summary.json).
- **Coverage.**
  - 72 lanes, 82 tables, 5,855 cells.
  - The other ~210 experiments report per event, run forward, or keep their numbers in text, and are not in this pass.
- **Groups** (1x costs; the 2x row, or gross − 2 × cost, when known):
  - robust: net > 0, t ≥ 2, still > 0 at 2x;
  - underpowered: net > 0, otherwise;
  - cost-killed: gross > 0, net ≤ 0;
  - negative.

**Read with care.**
- Every cell here was seen before. Taking the best cell is selection on seen data.
- Many lanes re-use the same book (allocation, optimizer, sizing, delay, regime and dispersion-gate all re-weight the
  flow books). So counts of cells are not counts of independent findings.
- A refinement is a new registered lane with its trials counted, and the decisive sample is one nobody has read.

### 1. The strongest repeated out-of-sample evidence is the flow family, book B
- **Book B** is `top_vs_crowd`: Binance's public top-trader positioning against the crowd's.
- **One-shot holdout, 2025-07..2026-09, 457 days.** About +66-71%/yr net (18-20 bp/day), Newey-West t 2.7, still
  positive at 2x and 3x costs.
  - R198 reported it without deciding on it, because its verdict was on book A (t 1.22).
  - R216 split it: crypto contracts +18.3 bp/day, t 2.74. Part of the gross sits in a few coins.
- **The same book reappears as the best holdout cell** in allocation (R228), optimizer (R253), sizing (R217), delay
  (R213), regime (R233) and the dispersion gate (R260). Those cells are not independent confirmations.
- **It is already in forward paper:**
  - R200 on Binance prices, from 2026-10-01;
  - R448 on Hyperliquid's crypto perps (reachable from France);
  - R451 on xyz stock perps;
  - R458 at Hyperliquid's measured costs.
  - Their reads are frozen (90 and 365 counted days). This scan does not read them.

### 1b. A second family out of sample: the lottery-cluster short with a squeeze cover (R298)
- **The book.** Short the quintile of small, young, volatile coins ("lottery" cluster). Each short is covered when it
  squeezes.
- **Validation, 24 h holds:** +16.4 to +18.1 bp/day (about +60 to +66%/yr), t 2.07-2.08, still +12.9 to +16.6 bp/day
  at 2x. The covered-minus-uncovered difference is +7.1 bp/day, t 2.23.
- **R298's verdict is NULL** under its own multiple-testing gate (a grid of score × hold × cover).
- It is a short book on small coins. R280 showed that such legs can be liquidated even at 1x, so margin and borrow are
  the open questions.

- **So the wide pass finds no positive family left unfollowed.** The decisions now sit on frozen forward dates.
- **History can still shorten the wait where a forward lane is slow.** Examples:
  - R469 reads once in 2027-02;
  - R266 needs 120 tickets.
  - A replay on another venue's unread history, frozen as its own lane, answers in days. The reserved samples
    (`docs/GATES.md`) have 2 of 4 reads left on 2020 and Gate, and 3 of 4 on KuCoin, dYdX, Lighter and MEXC-only coins.
- **Power check for the reserved 2020 sample.** For the lottery-cluster short (§1b), one year of 81 coins is too
  little.
  - At +16 bp/day and a daily sd near 300 bp, it gives t ≈ 1 before any Bonferroni.
  - A read there would spend a scarce batch on a futility test. The multi-year long-tail samples have more power.

## What this changes in the plan
1. **Data first.** The container was replaced on 2026-11-17 and `data/` was lost. It is being rebuilt:
   - the recordings: `data-archive`, 39,265 files, every sha256 checked;
   - the Binance USD-M public archive: klines, funding, premium index, mark price and metrics, checked against
     `docs/provenance/*.json`;
   - then the venue histories by priority (Gate, MEXC, Hyperliquid, KuCoin, dYdX, Lighter, Kraken, spot, COIN-M).
2. **Refine book B where the remaining question is**:
   - execution on a France-reachable venue;
   - its cost when traded with confidence and cost levers.
   - A counted grid on the 2021-2026 archive is in-sample (the holdout is spent), so it can only rank variants. The
     decisive sample is the forward data since 2026-10, which R448/R458 already freeze.
   - Any new variant is frozen before its own forward days.
3. **Underpowered positives.** More data before any new grid: longer histories and other venues, read on reserved
   samples where they exist.
4. **Cost-killed cells.** Re-price with measured maker fills (R498's read after 2026-12-01) before any new lane.
5. **The lottery-cluster short (R298).** Its validation edge survives 2x. What would decide it:
   - fresh data after its window (Binance archive from 2026-10, rebuilt now);
   - a margin and borrow check on the venues reachable from France.
