## 1. What "conclusive" means here
A result is conclusive when all five hold:
1. **It is measured on data nobody chose the rule on.** That means forward days, or a reserved sample read once.
2. **It is measured at the cost and size one would trade.**
3. **Its power was stated honestly before the read.** A FAIL is conclusive only if a real effect would probably have
   passed.
4. **It is read once, on a fixed date, by a frozen rule.**
5. **It answers a decision someone will take.**

A FAIL that meets these five is as conclusive as a PASS. "Probant" does not mean "positive".

## 2. Why the evidence has not been conclusive so far
1. **Many questions, none tied to a decision.**
   - There are 277 registered rounds; 104 rows were added in November alone.
   - 70 rows are open. A census of their frozen PREREGs (2026-11-17) gives, by stated power at the first look:
     - 2 HIGH;
     - 11 MEDIUM;
     - 38 LOW;
     - 19 measurements with no pass bar.
   - Both HIGH rows rest on windows of the same archive that picked the rule. **No open row reaches 70% power at a
     realistic forward effect.**
2. **Forward returns are slow by arithmetic, not by bad luck.**
   - **The formula.** With independent daily returns, the years needed for 80% power at the lanes' bar (t ≥ 2.5) are
     about (3.34 / annual Sharpe)².
   - **Binance + Gate.** R437 calls the v1 engine "the fairer capital-neutral number" for this book. On 2021-2026 that
     gives Sharpe 2.4, so about 1.9 years. The v2 engine gives Sharpe 1.5, so about 5 years (appendix).
   - **Why the stated power is higher.** The lanes' PREREGs give much higher pass shares (section 3). Those are the
     shares of past 90-day windows that passed, which is a different thing from power:
     - the windows overlap, so the 2025-26 sample holds only about 5 to 7 independent 90-day blocks;
     - each book was the best of R275's 560;
     - funding comes in bursts.
   - **What that means for look 1.** On 2025+ days, R437's v1 Sharpe for binance+gate implies an expected 90-day t of
     about 1.3 under a constant effect. Yet R390 states that 51% of 90-day windows reached 2.5. **So a first look
     largely asks whether the next 90 days hold a strong funding regime.**
   - Adding similar lanes does not fix this: they share the same funding regime and the same legs.
3. **Costs were measured after signals.** Most nulls were real effects smaller than their execution cost. Charter
   rule 2 (2026-11-16) now puts cost first.
4. **Writers were lost.**
   - The container stop of 2026-11-06 left 12 rows without a writer.
   - It also cost the carry books on-time days that can never be recovered (section 4).
   - GitHub Actions has been the only runner since 2026-11-10.
5. **The one premium that held was on venues outside scope** until the owner put them back on 2026-11-16.
6. **Re-slicing the same archive adds trials, not evidence.** The program counts 9,785,313 trials. Each new cut of
   2021-2026 raises the bar for every later result.

## 3. The decision
**Should the owner put a small real stake into cross-venue funding carry? If so, on which book, at what size, and
with which orders?**

- **What the archive says.**
  - R437 audited seven books on 2021-2026, and all seven SURVIVE.
  - For binance+gate:
    - v1 gives +10.05%/yr at 1x, NW t 5.96;
    - v2 gives +13.42%/yr, NW t 3.48, max drawdown −6.7%;
    - its top ten days made 49% of the gain.
  - About 3.5 points a year of v2's price P&L come from "blowout" exits, where the two legs' prices moved more than

- **The decisive data arrive between 2027-01-20 and 2027-02-09.** Until then the lab's job is twofold:
  - make sure the data arrive clean;
  - make sure a PASS can be acted on at once.

## 5. Make a PASS actionable before it happens
**The owner decides two things before 2027-01-15.** The lab cannot take these decisions.
1. **Venues.** On which of Binance, Gate, KuCoin and MEXC can you hold a futures account under that venue's own
   rules? Hyperliquid and Lighter need only a wallet.
   - A PASS on a book whose venues you cannot hold is evidence, not a trade. Each PREREG says so.
   - The lab does not help get around a venue's identity or country rules.
2. **Stake.** The most you would accept to lose on a first real trial. GATES gate 4 proposes:
   - at most 2% of trading capital per book;
   - a hard stop at 25% of the stake.
   - Those are proposals; the stake is yours.

**Agent work: a small-stake plan, frozen before 2027-01-20.**
- It gets its own registry row. It is a measurement, with no return trials.
- It applies only to a book that qualifies, and it fixes the following before any look.

*Which book qualifies:*
1. The book PASSes under its own rule, at look 1 or look 2.
2. It is one of the seven pre-committed books. R278 C, picked after reading R275, never qualifies.
3. No more than 3 of those seven are FAIL at that time.
   - FAIL here means a look-1 FAIL (mean ≤ 0), a look-2 FAIL, or the kill switch.
   - CONTINUE is not FAIL.
   - This rule only adds caution and changes no lane's verdict. It costs some power when a real edge meets a weak
     regime. It is kept because the books share legs, and a false start costs real money.
4. **The cross-lane bar is reported beside every PASS.**
   - 7 books × 2 looks make 14 one-sided tests, so Bonferroni at 0.05 needs z ≥ 2.69.
   - Each lane's 2.5 corrects only within that lane. A PASS between 2.5 and 2.69 is reported as such.
5. **A PASS labelled degraded timing still qualifies.** Its label is reported. The stake itself measures what late
   days miss: a real fill against the paper.
6. **If several books qualify:**
   - first, the books whose venues the owner can hold;
   - among those, the earliest PASS; a tie goes to the higher NW t.
   - One book at a time.

*Gates 2 and 3 for these venues.* GATES writes them for MEXC; the plan states each venue's contract rules and
measured costs in their place:
- **Sizes.** Market orders sized so each leg stays inside its measured cost. For C14 that is $1k a leg
  ([fee map](../../research/intake/fee_map_20261116.md)), about $40k of capital at K = 20. A small stake is well
  inside it.
- **Limits set before any result is read:**
  - the gross cap;
  - the per-contract cap;
  - the daily stop and the account stop.
- **The margin and liquidation replay** on the paper record.
- **No leverage above 1x:** R280 found no safe leverage.
- **The `lab.gate.go_real()` input:** each paper ticket's unlevered net, from entry to exit, scored at the lane's own
  day count and never earlier (GATES).

*What the paper does not model.* Each item gets a handling rule, or a stop:
- collateral moves between venues;
- a withdrawal or account freeze;
- liquidation or auto-deleveraging of one leg;
- a fill on one leg only;
- funding settled at hours other than the 00:00 close;
- delisting exits;
- the Lighter bridge for R294.
- Tax is the owner's matter.

*What the stake measures:*
- each real fill against the paper ticket at the same decision;
- each funding payment received against the paper's.
- These are paired differences, so they settle fast.
  - In R390's first live days, about one pair was entered or exited each day, two legs each.
  - Up to 20 held pairs collected funding several times a day.
  - That is enough to settle the real-versus-paper gap within one to two months, not years.

*How data reaches the lab:*
- The owner exports the venue's trade history weekly.
- No key and no order ever passes through the lab.
- Any order before a PASS, even a tiny calibration order, would be a carve-out from AGENTS rule 6. Only the owner can
  grant it, and this plan assumes none.

## 8. What this plan does not do
- **It changes no read.** It does not shorten any read, change any threshold or pool books into a new read.
- **It does not promise a PASS.**
  - Each first look is at best about even odds if the edge is real.
  - R390 and R440 are lower, because their drawdown condition binds.
  - A weak funding regime in the next 90 days lowers all of them.
- **It does not move money.** Real money still needs:
  1. a forward PASS;
  2. `lab.gate.go_real()`;
  3. gates 2 and 3 written down;
  4. stakes placed by the owner.

## 9. Revision, same evening: two speeds, and the real-money rule is the owner's
**The owner's objection.** Past years can be used, 2027 will look like earlier years, and live paper trading should
not be the only proof. The [room](room_haiku_20261117.md) agreed with the principle and with the diagnosis:
- **Forward paper cannot prove the return.** The expected t at 90 days is about 1.2 to 1.3 at a constant effect.
- **History has established the premium as well as it can.** That rests on 2021-2026 across venues, 2020 read blind,
  and long tails read blind.
- **What history cannot show is execution.** That covers real fills, funding actually credited, transfers,
  delistings and outages. For these venues there is almost no order-book history before 2026-10.

The room also corrected the earlier sections on two points:
- **Expect less.** Blind replications on long tails earned +0.7 to +2.5%/yr (R391, R392), against +6 to +16%/yr for
  the books picked on 2021-2026. Selection inflated the headline figures. Plan for a few percent a year, not ten.
- **Fixed costs count.** Small-stake plans must also price transfers, withdrawals and collateral moves. At small size,
  these can exceed the carry.

**What changes:**

| Speed | What | When | What it decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks | R498 read: real queue and fill rate of a resting Gate order | 2026-12-01..07 | The order type, and whether the maker route is worth it |
| Weeks | **A measurement pilot** (below), if the owner adds the exception to rule 6 | after R498's read and the pilot's frozen plan | Whether real fills and funding match paper. It is the first true execution test |
| Weeks | A replication on a venue never read for carry, where its public history is reachable from here | after a PREREG frozen before the fetch | Whether the premium generalises (gate 1 "another venue" in GATES). It is not a validation of the seven books |
| Months | The eight forward books, each read on its own dates (section 3) | 2027-01-20..05-10 | Mechanics and regime. They are the gate to **scaling** past the pilot, no longer to the first small stake |

**Venues probed for that replication (one request each, never retried):**
- refused from this container: Bybit and BitMEX (country block);
- answering: Bitfinex (derivatives history from at least 2021-08) and OKX (its API keeps 3 months of funding).

**The measurement pilot** (a proposal; its plan is frozen before any order):
- **Size.** A few pairs, at least one leg at the paper size of $250 to $1k, so that slippage and minimum order sizes
  show.
- **Placement.** Placed by the owner on accounts the owner holds, with keys that cannot withdraw. Agents never place
  orders.
- **What judges it:** execution, not profit. Specifically:
  - each fill against the paper ticket;
  - funding credited against the published rate;
  - transfers and their costs;
  - outages and missed days.
- **Before it starts:**
  - a written delisting procedure;
  - a check that the book stays net-positive at 2x and 3x costs without its blowout exits;
  - alerts on the runner.
- **Limits:**
  - a hard stop fixed in advance;
  - no increase of the stake on paper results alone.

**Rule 6 of AGENTS.md is the owner's, and only the owner changes it.** Two options:
1. **Keep it as written.** Real money waits for a forward PASS (2027-01-20 at the earliest), and the pilot plan is
   frozen before that.
2. **Add an exception for a measurement pilot.** Proposed text, to append to rule 6:
   > *Exception (owner, date): one measurement pilot of at most $X of capital may start before a forward PASS. Its plan
   > is frozen and registered first. It is placed by the owner and judged on execution only. It never scales without
   > a forward PASS.*
