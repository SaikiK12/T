## What came out

### 1. Time: what can and cannot be cut
- **The estimand split** (agent 3). It is the most useful framing of the evening.
  - *Cost per fill* can be pinned in days from recorded books.
  - *Alpha* cannot: telling ±1%/yr apart at 3% volatility needs decades.
  - So: replace forward waiting with history wherever the question is execution. Accept that the return question is
    settled by long samples plus out-of-sample books, not by a few more months of paper.
- **Recorded books that exist today, free.**
  - Gate's public S3 archive: every USDT perp's book, hour by hour, from 2021-07, no key. Verified by the lead.
  - Tardis.dev's free first-day-of-month files: Binance futures from 2019-11, Gate from 2020-07, OKX swaps from
    2019-03, Bitfinex derivatives from 2019-09, KuCoin futures from 2024-01, Hyperliquid from 2024-10. Verified by the
    lead.
    - Their terms allow internal research and aggregates of 10 minutes or more.
    - They bar redistributing raw files, so raw files are never committed.
- **Owner's decisions, not taken here:**
  - a Tardis trial (10 days, 20 GB) needs an account;
  - Hyperliquid's L2 archive is requester-pays on AWS and needs an AWS account.

- **Funding decay.** Binance BTC funding was 11.9 / 5.1 / 3.1%/yr in 2024 / 2025 / 2026 to date; ETH 13.0 / 4.9 / 2.0
  (agent 5, from the Binance archive).
  - sUSDe yield followed: 14.8 / 6.5 / 3.7% (DefiLlama, no key).
  - The CME 3-month basis has been under the 2-year Treasury yield since 2026-02 (Glassnode, via press).
- **What this means for us.** Our books are *cross-venue* spreads, not spot-perp.
  - R390's own table shows 2025-01..2026-09 still at about +17.5%/yr at 1x.
  - So cross-venue dispersion has not decayed the way the BTC funding level has. That is a claim to keep testing,
    not a reassurance.
