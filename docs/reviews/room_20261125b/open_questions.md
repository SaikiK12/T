## Q01. Does Variational's frozen weekend index leave a gap that closes at the reopen? (NEW)

- **Hypothesis H1:** over weekends, the spread between Variational's stock perp mark and trade.xyz's mark is above 100 bp
  on at least 20% of weekends, and it closes within two hours of the US open.
  **H0:** the spread is within 20 bp on at least 75% of weekends (the OLP re-quotes the frozen index, or the frozen
  index tracks the book).
- **Why it is not known.** The synthesis verified the freeze from the source: "Fixed Mode (Daily Maintenance, Holidays
  and Weekends): Fixed Mode is applied on Equity-based RWA Perps. No recalculation occurs, the Price Index remains
  fixed at the last available value" (`docs/reviews/room_20261125/synthesis.md:90`). No registry row mentions
  Variational (grep `variational` over `registry/methods.csv` and `rounds.csv` returns nothing). The recorder started
  on 2026-11-25 (`scripts/record_variational.py`, per synthesis).
- **Mechanism.** The index is frozen, so a single-maker OLP prices off a stale anchor. If xyz moves by W over the weekend
  while Variational stays put, the two venues disagree until the open. The gap is paid by whoever holds the wrong side
  of the OLP's quote. Barrier: the OLP's last look and weekend risk limits, a split margin across two venues, and
  access.
- **Data.** Held: `data/xyz_wknd` (trade.xyz weekend books from R462). Recording from 2026-11-25 in the Variational
  folder of branch `claude/xyz-books-data`. Public: Variational `/metadata/v2/stats` (10 requests per 10 s, read-only,
  marks and indicative quotes at $1k, $100k, $1m).
- **Cheapest experiment.** Read the two recorded weekends (11-28/29) and the Thanksgiving holiday (11-26) from the
  recorder, against `xyz_wknd`. Frozen rule written first. Cost: about 2 h of my work, EUR 0. Gate Q28 first: the read
  endpoint is public, but the account trade path may be closed to a French resident.
- **Kill result.** The spread is inside 20 bp on at least 75% of the weekends with |W| > 100 bp, or it never exceeds
  50 bp on any weekend.
- **Value if true.** Assume 20 qualifying weekends a year, a EUR 3k leg each, and a net edge of 40 bp:
  20 × 3,000 × 0.004 = EUR 240 a year, 2.4% of EUR 10k. Before capture by the OLP's limits.
- **Score.** 240 × 0.25 ÷ 2 = 30.

## Q03. Does Kraken's mark lag the index on alt longs during a wick? (PARTLY)

- **Hypothesis H1:** on alt perps, Kraken's mark sits below Binance's mark for a long account by more than 5% for at
  least 10 minutes during a wick, so the long is liquidated on Kraken and not on Binance.
  **H0:** the marks agree within 1% during wicks.
- **Why it is not known.** The mechanism_map row reads "2025-10-10: Kraken's mark low averaged −43.8% over 126 coins
  (Binance prints −65% to −69%)" (`docs/reviews/mechanism_map.md:13`). That is a level, not a lag. R252 fades
  liquidation-cascade proxies on top-50 perps (`rounds.csv`), not the mark formula. R280 and M265 test leverage and
  liquidation, not the mark.
- **Mechanism.** Kraken's mark is the spot index plus an EMA30s of (impact mid − index), premium capped at 1%. The EMA
  lags the wick, and the cap binds during the wick. Those two facts make the mark lower than the index for longs.
- **Data.** Held: `data/kraken_passive/mark1h` (PF_*USD parquet, Kraken marks) and `data/kraken_hist/contracts.csv`. The
  index needs the public index series, to be checked.
- **Cheapest experiment.** 2025-10-10 and two other cascades (2024-08-05, 2026 events from the lab's stress list in
  M324). Compare Kraken mark with Binance mark at 1-minute resolution where available, 1-hour otherwise. Cost 3 h, EUR 0.
- **Kill result.** The mark gap is under 1% in the 30 minutes around each wick.
- **Value if true.** One long of EUR 2.5k at 2x liquidated early costs its margin, about EUR 500. P(such a wick in a
  year) about 0.3 means a risk of EUR 150 a year, which is the value of a stop or a leverage cut.
- **Score.** 500 × 0.15 ÷ 3 = 25 (V = EUR 500 is the loss per event; P is the chance the answer changes the leverage).

## Q19. What is the euro P&L of the dollar legs, and how does it move with EUR/USD? (NEW)

- **Hypothesis H1:** the euro-denominated P&L of the USDC legs has a EUR/USD beta of at least 0.5 on a monthly basis,
  so a euro investor carries a dollar exposure that a hedge removes. **H0:** the beta is below 0.2.
- **Why it is not known.** `docs/reviews/answers_20261124.md:54` raises the currency risk ("Collateral in USDC is a
  dollar position. For a euro investor its EUR/USD swing…") and marks the hedge "a later refinement" (line 60). The
  synthesis lists it as open ("G2… Open as a measurement"). No round measures it. M216 (dollar cycle) is about USD
  strength, not a euro investor's P&L.
- **Mechanism.** The P&L is the USD return of the legs, converted at the spot rate. Only the dollar-denominated part is
  exposed. Kraken multi-collateral takes euros, so its principal is in euros and only the P&L is dollar.
- **Data.** EUR/USD daily: Yahoo FX (M216 source, free, a few MB, to fetch). Book equity by month: the R525 results in
  `experiments/gap_breadth_v1/results/pair_daily_net_1x.csv` (read the file, not the journal).
- **Cheapest experiment.** Regress the monthly euro return of the book on the monthly EUR/USD return, 2021-2026. 2 h, EUR
  0.
- **Kill result.** Beta below 0.2, or its 95% interval contains zero.
