# Sizing the carry books for a small stake (2026-11-20)

- **Asked by the owner (2026-11-20):** "do everything" after the library digest, which ranked sizing second.
- **What this is.** Input to the owner's decision. It is no order, and it is not a frozen plan: a small stake still
  needs a PASS from R510 or R512 and a frozen plan through `lab.gate.go_real()`.
- **The tool:** `lab/sizing.py`, tested in `tests/test_sizing.py`.
  - Risk-constrained Kelly (Busseti, Ryu & Boyd 2016): maximise E log(1 + L r) subject to E[(1 + L r)^(−λ)] ≤ 1,
    with λ = ln β / ln α. With α = 0.8 and β = 0.05 (λ = 13.4), it bounds the chance that wealth ever falls 20% below
    its start by 5%.
  - A simulation test checks that bound.
- **Capital convention.** As the lab's books state returns: on two-leg capital, so at leverage L each leg's notional
  is L times the collateral its venue holds for it.

## Inputs
- **Returns.** R508's and R511's daily nets at their 2x and 3x cost lines.
  - R513 and the live snapshots put real costs near 2-3x the 7 bp test cost.
  - The edge is shrunk to 40% or 60% of its in-sample mean before sizing; deviations are kept. The program-level
    Tweedie shrinkage of R511 was 0.40-0.96 (`research/intake/large_scale_inference_20261119.md`).
- **Liquidation, two ways.** Computed from the coins the books held, with Kraken and Hyperliquid daily highs and lows.
  Aster's stored candles hold closes only, so its legs use close-to-close moves, an underestimate.

  | Margin | Worst case | Leverage at which a leg (or venue) is liquidated |
  |---|---|---|
  | Isolated per leg | a single coin's adverse intraday excursion: 34.8% at the 99th percentile, 81.7% at the 99.9th, 526% at worst (27,369 coin-days) | 1.2x at the 99.9th percentile |
  | Cross margin per venue, 20 legs | the day's sum of every held leg's adverse excursion, all assumed at their worst at once (an upper bound): R508 max 12.5% (Kraken) and 14.9% (Hyperliquid); R511 max 11.4% (Kraken) and 9.0% (Aster) | 6.5x (R508), 8.5x (R511), with a 3% maintenance margin |

## Results
| Book | Cost line | Shrink | Risk-constrained Kelly | Liquidation cap (cross margin) | Half of the smaller | Shrunk %/yr at 1x |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R508 | 2x | 0.4 | 11.0x | 6.5x | 3.2x | 9.0 |
| R508 | 2x | 0.6 | 16.7x | 6.5x | 3.2x | 13.5 |
| R508 | 3x | 0.4 | 10.0x | 6.5x | 3.2x | 8.3 |
| R511 (31 new coins) | 2x | 0.4 | 6.3x | 8.5x | 3.2x | 4.7 |
| R511 | 3x | 0.4 | 5.4x | 8.5x | 2.7x | 4.1 |

- **What binds.** Under cross margin, liquidation binds before growth on R508; risk-constrained Kelly binds on R511.
- **Isolated margin would cap the books near 0.6x.** A single thin coin's spike would liquidate an isolated leg. This
  confirms R280's rule: cross margin per venue, never isolated legs.
- **Expected return.** Half the smaller cap is about 3x. At 3x, the shrunk edge would give roughly 25-40%/yr on
  capital for R508's book at 2x costs. This is before venue failure and before the costs R513 can remove.

## A proposal for the first stake (the owner decides)
- **Size:**
  - at most $2,000 of capital, as R510 allows after a PASS;
  - cross margin on each venue;
  - book leverage at most 2x;
  - 20 slots of about $100 notional a leg.
- **Why 2x and not 3x:**
  - ESMA's 2026 statement makes EU retail caps on perpetuals likely (2:1 for crypto CFDs).
  - The first stake measures fills and frictions, not return.
- **The stop.** Close all pairs if cumulative P&L falls below −3% of capital (R509/R512's kill switch), or if either
  venue changes its funding formula or delists most held coins.
- **Not covered by any of these numbers:**
  - a venue failing or freezing withdrawals, which loses the collateral held there;
  - correlated squeezes larger than this year's;
  - funding-formula changes;
  - the owner's tax treatment.
  - Keep the stake to money the owner can lose.
