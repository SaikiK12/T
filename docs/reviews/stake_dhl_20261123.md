# Leverage and margin for a Deribit + Hyperliquid design-gap stake, 2026-11-23

- **What this is.** Money readiness for Bet 1 (`docs/reviews/breakthrough_20261121.md`, item 4), ahead of R521's first
  look (about 2026-12-22).
- **It decides nothing.** A stake still needs a PASS and `lab.gate.go_real()`, placed by the owner.

## Tool
- `lab/margin.py` (tests in `tests/test_margin.py`):
  - `liq_move`: the uniform adverse move that liquidates one venue's cross-margin account;
  - `split`: the equity split between venues that equalises both moves;
  - `rebalance`: the transfer that restores a target distance.
- **Maintenance rates assumed:**
  - Hyperliquid at half its initial margin at max leverage: BTC 1.25%, ETH 2%, 10x alts 5%;
  - Deribit's rates were not read, so they are assumed the same.

## Stress, measured (`scripts/stress_dhl.py`, Deribit hourly closes, 2023-06-01..2026-11-19)
- **The basket:** an equal-weight basket of 15 liquid coins (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, AVAX, ADA, DOT, ALGO, BCH, DOGE, LINK,
  LTC, NEAR, TRX, UNI).
  - Worst 24 h: −24.2% / +20.9%. Worst 72 h: −28.9% / +33.5%.
- **Single coins:** worst 24 h down to −40% (ALGO), up to +86% (TRX).
  - With 20 slots on cross margin, one coin is about 5% of a venue's notional. A single-coin spike moves the account
    by a few percent; a market-wide move is what liquidates.

| Leverage (total notional / capital) | Move to liquidation, BTC-like | 10x alts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 99% | 95% |
| 2 | 49% | 45% |
| 3 | 32% | 28% |
| 4 | 24% | 20% |
| 5 | 19% | 15% |

## Rule proposed for a first stake
- **Leverage at most 2.5** (each leg's notional 1.25x capital), with capital split by `margin.split`.
  - Distance to liquidation is then about 35%, about 1.5x the worst 24 h basket move.
  - That holds with rebalancing once a day.
- **Alerts and actions:**
  - rebalance when either venue's distance falls under 30% (`margin.rebalance` to 35%);
  - cut both legs pro rata if a transfer cannot restore 25%.
- **What it would earn.** R519's reported book was +4.5%/yr at leverage 1, so about +11%/yr at 2.5, before
  Hyperliquid's idle-USDC yield. That is an in-sample figure, which R520/R521 must confirm.

## Not checked
- Deribit's maintenance schedule, which may be tiered by size.
- Liquidation during an exchange outage or an oracle gap.
- Transfer delays between venues. A rebalance needs a withdrawal and a deposit, so the daily cadence assumes both
  clear within hours.

## Added 2026-11-23 after R525's audit: the tail is a broken hedge
- **The event.** The one-day-delay audit found LUNA (May 2022): one hedged trade lost about 34x a leg's notional
  before a daily check could close it.
- **Before any stake:**
  - an hourly ratio-divergence stop per pair (close both legs above about 10% from entry);
  - a cap of about 5% of book capital per coin;
  - no entry on coins with a venue halt, delisting notice or dead series;
  - minimum printing activity on both venues.
- These limits sit on top of the leverage cap above.

## Priced 2026-11-23 (R526, `experiments/risk_limits_v1/FINDINGS.md`)
- **Entry filters do the work.**
  - Price agreement at entry: with the one-day delay, it lifts the 12-pair portfolio from +4.6 to +7.2%/yr, and its
    worst day from −14.3% to −0.4%. At the base timing it costs nothing.
  - Printing activity on both legs: on the 4 hourly pairs, Sharpe 3.4 → 4.6 and max drawdown −2.8% → −0.7%.
- **The hourly stop on last-trade prices is dropped.** 92% of Bitfinex stops reverted within a day, and some
  "filled" at outlier prints. Max drawdown doubled.
- **Live form:**
  - agreement on book mids, normalized by the legs' usual ratio (for 1000x contracts);
  - a two-sided book with depth on both legs;
  - the engine's daily 20% blowout and dead-leg exits.
