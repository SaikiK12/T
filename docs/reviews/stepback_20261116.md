## 1. What the project knows (the views agree; checked)
1. **Costs decide.** Most effects found here are real but smaller than the cost of trading them.
   - R287: gross +18.8 bp a day against 18-76 bp of cost.
   - R300: +72 bp a day against a 34 bp round trip.
   - The 2026-11-04 audit recomputed the "PASS" settlement hops R354, R356 and R363 at measured cost: −26.2, −6.6 and
     −35.9 bp a hop ([EDGE_SCOREBOARD](../EDGE_SCOREBOARD.md), item 1).
   - This is the measured answer to the owner's "why do you always talk about fees?".
2. **Funding carry is the one premium that held on data nobody had read**, but on venues that do not serve French
   residents with perpetuals: Binance, Gate, KuCoin and MEXC ([venue access](venue_access_fr_20261110.md)).
   - On venues usable from France, a DEX-only carry book earned below the bill on seen rows.
   - Other yields (HLP/LLP vault deposits, hedged JLP/GM, HIP-3 perp carry) looked good on seen 2026 data and are in
     forward test. They are candidates, not results.
   - Measured book costs (OpenHands, R495, PR #100, unmerged, not re-run here) put the break-even of the C14 carry book
     near $1k-$5k per leg.
3. **Speed games are closed from here.** With public data, about 250 ms to the venues and no keys, R347, R349, R367
   and R431 were NULL or blocked.
4. **Ideas from social media have not survived.** 40 queue rows came from reels or TikToks, and every one that reached
   the scoreboard is NULL.
5. **The process works as a filter.**
   - It caught a leak: R304's +58.9 bp a day was a data artefact (R332).
   - It sank the unlock short on reserved data (R286) after it looked like the best edge.
   - It demoted its own CONFIRMED rows to in-sample (`docs/GATES.md`).
   - It keeps every null on record.

**What it has only accumulated:**
- 444 queue rows, 69 open registry rows, 274 lane folders and 62 top-level documents.
- A 900-line DIRECTION whose "Now" is dated 2026-10-28.
- 102 new registry rows in the first 16 days of November, while forward decisions arrive in 2027. The lab starts
  faster than it decides.

**The 9,785,313 "program trials" mislead as a headline.**
- Five rounds hold 9.75 million of them; R192 alone holds 8.3 million.
- The median round has 3 trials (`registry/rounds.csv`).
- It is a bookkeeping total, not a measure of rigour or of how many ideas were tried.

## 2. Where the reviewers disagreed, and the checked answer
- **D said carry "does not clear costs at the owner's size".**
  - On C14's venues the measurement says the opposite at small size: it clears cost at $1k-$5k per leg and fails at
    $10k.
  - The obstacle D missed is that those venues are closed to French residents.
- **A said more idea-hunting "almost certainly has negative expected value".** Kept as a direction, not a ban.
  - The owner asked earlier not to drop ideas without research.
  - So the rule is about sequencing: a new hunt is justified when it changes a binding constraint (cost, size,
    access). Another signal under the same constraints does not qualify.
- **B called 2026-11-19 a "cliff".**
  - Codex's R420 runtime was frozen to end its dispatch then, and after that only reports health
    (`docs/OPERATIONS.md`). Nothing breaks.
  - The four hourly `quant-*` workflows then need retiring, by the runtime's own route.
- **B proposed four new files: claims, liveness, a generated status page and an archive index. A and C asked for
  fewer documents.** The resolution:
  - one hand-kept status page, rewritten in place;
  - a read-only census computed on demand, so no new journal;
  - written coordination rules.
- **C read README's "MEXC perps first" and README's own note that MEXC does not serve French residents as a
  contradiction.** It was one; README now names venues usable from France.

## 3. The position
1. **Change the question.**
   - From "find an edge" to "what is the best use of a small capital and of a scarce owner's attention, given what is
     now known?".
   - Today's answer: no strategy has passed the gates, so the default is no trade. The bill rate is the hurdle any
     strategy must beat.
   - Let the forward lanes on venues usable from France finish. Open new work only where it moves a binding constraint.
2. **Pre-registration is a discipline only if decisions arrive.**
   - A new lane that cannot decide within about a year at a realistic effect is registered as monitoring. Frozen lanes
     keep their scope.
   - A "PASS recorded PARTIAL" on data its author had seen is a reason to test, not evidence.
3. **What the project produces.**
   - Honest knowledge, cheaply kept, of what works and what does not.
   - A short list of candidates being tested forward on venues the owner can use. Their first futility reads are
     2027-02; the first possible pass is 2027-05.
   - The nulls are not a verdict on trading in general. They say which constraint binds (cost, then access), and that
     is where the next work goes.

## 6. What the fifth reviewer changed
E's critique is in [`stepback_20261116/E_critic_sonnet.md`](stepback_20261116/E_critic_sonnet.md). Its points, and what
was done with each:
1. **"Closing rows has no legal mechanism"** (high). Accepted.
   - `lab/registry.py` only appends, and AGENTS rule 3 bars hand edits.
   - A row's status changes only with its frozen read, as R392's did.
   - A lane whose writer is gone gets a dated "**Writer lost**" note in its README, and the census counts it.
   - Whether to add an append-only status-event journal is left for later, and only if notes prove too weak.
2. **"Freed ids collide with frozen PREREGs"** (high). Accepted: an id used on any branch is never reused.
3. **"Carry is overstated as the only premium"** (high). Accepted.
   - The French-usable DEX-only carry book was below the bill.
   - Vault and HIP-3 yields are named as candidates.
   - The bill is named as the hurdle.
4. **"From 2027-02" is futility only** (medium). Accepted: the first possible pass is 2027-05.
5. **Census wording** (medium). Accepted.
   - R419 is listed.
   - R369 and R286 are described accurately.
   - "workflow" means "named and recently committed", not proof that every run succeeded.
6. **"The cadence has no scheduler"** (medium). Accepted.
   - The check runs at each session, or weekly if the owner enables the prepared task.
   - Automated commits never notify; Claude's note is at most weekly.
   - The open drafts already breach the 7-day rule. Their integration is the first upkeep item.
7. **"Keep capital in bills" is advice beyond the rules** (medium). Accepted. It became "no strategy has passed, so the
   default is no trade; the bill is the hurdle".
8. **"Leans toward despair"** (medium). Accepted in part.
   - The dollar arithmetic is removed.
   - The candidates are named.
   - The nulls are read as a map of binding constraints.
9. **The root cause of the e-mails is unfixed** (low). Stated in section 4. Moving bot journals off the default
   branch relocates paths that frozen lanes and readers resolve. That is a migration, not a tweak, and is not done
   here.
10. **Dangling link, README line 4, workflow counts** (low). The link now exists. README line 4 was already changed,
    which E's read missed. The count is 24 `claude-*` workflows of 30.
11. **Missed by everyone.** All four points were taken up:
    - the check now flags writers that stopped (`workflow-quiet`), and dated reads are no longer mistaken for them;
    - STATUS has a French summary;
    - "monitoring" applies to new lanes only;
    - every follow-up item is bounded to one session.
