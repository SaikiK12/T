## Question and finite inputs
For one explicitly reported partial or complete fill, calculate signed `quantity*(future_mid-fill_price)` and decompose it exactly once into initial spread component and signed midpoint movement. Reuse `lab.paper_account.PaperEvent`/`PaperAccount` on segregated capital for an explicitly hypothetical exit at the horizon midpoint, not an actual execution; separately deduct an explicitly assumed paid-action charge. The fill and quotes carry source/receive clocks. Accept a quote only if received by as-of, in the same venue/symbol, known by fill time, within the fixed initial age and horizon-lag limits; reject clock drift, absent final reconciliation, malformed counts and collateral shortfall. Accepted ALO does not itself imply a fill or priority. Missing final order states are UNKNOWN; complete no-fill reports retain their actual assumed action cost and have no per-fill denominator.

Four fixed constructed cases: a single $1000 filled-notional long with 100 accounted actions paid; the identical cash case with zero paid actions (the initial free allowance is finite, not a permanent rebate); 100 paid attempts with no fill; and one unresolved terminal order. The source fee inputs are 1.5 bp per leg from the publicly displayed Hyperliquid base tier; the exit at midpoint with a passive fee is an intentionally optimistic hypothetical requiring an unobserved fill. $0.0005 per paid action is the owner's scenario, not verified by the fetched rate-limit page; actual paid requests and tier are unknown. No funding, inventory/tail charge or exit slippage is assumed measured. These idealizations prevent any actual net or capacity claim.

For each of the four cases apply all fees and action/extra costs at fixed 1x, 2x and 3x: twelve diagnostic cells, zero predictive/trading variants. Use the same $2000 segregated capital and fixed quantity; do not resize, add spread again, drop inactivity, infer unknown fills, or call the result live PnL. Report full signed markout, spread and move, actual assumed costs, net component and per-capital component where computable. $1 per cumulative USDC traded is a documented address allowance with finite buffer and congestion limits, not a guarantee of access.

## Validation and limits
Run the committed implementation only after committing this spec, all code, fixture and source identities. An independent Fraction implementation checks the 12 cases and every reported decomposition. Repeat output bytes in a distinct path. Unit tests check long/short and partial quantity, timing, stale and wrong-venue data, unknown/rejected states, segregated-capital shortfall and 1x/2x/3x scaling. No order, private account, large download, historical/market acquisition, source-profit replication, tested alpha or prospective scoring is authorized by this diagnostic. The real necessary inputs are first-seen synchronized cross-venue L4/depth and reference prices; observable private acceptance/cancel/fill receipts, empirical arrival/cancel latency, queue and partial fills; current account fee/access, funding, risk and capacity. Without them actual conditional fill markout and positive expected net remain UNKNOWN.
