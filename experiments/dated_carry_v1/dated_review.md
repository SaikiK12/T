## Source mechanics and fixed quantities

The source's inverse USD-face amount is distinct from coin quantity and contract count. Metadata min_trade_amount is both order minimum and amount increment; contract_size converts to contract count and does not replace that step. The adapter pins future/reversed/coin-settled/USD identity, requested month expiry and book state open rather than visibility alone. Both sides must have finite, sorted, positive depth; each shown USD amount respects the source increment. Published millisecond clock is retained without claiming matching-event time. Tick-size steps, MEXC base quantity/actual lot constraints, owner fees and transfer route remain explicitly unverified; executable admission and actual net stay false/unknown.

Fixed face is floored to the declared USD amount step before comparing controls. Full-depth 3x adverse entry prices determine the reciprocal hedge plus 3x coin-entry-fee reserve. That same reference coin quantity is rounded UP to the spot metadata decimal-precision convention. This is explicitly not proof of actual MEXC quantity step. Quantity stays fixed across full/top capacity profiles, fee controls, cost tiers and terminal worlds; insufficient capacity remains unavailable instead of shrinking face. Both entry and diagnostic current exit must fit shown spot capacity and source minimum/maximum amounts; a current exit is not a future fill guarantee.

A literal invented book has USD-face fills100at110 and150at100, future midpoint115. At3x entry spread these prices become100 and70; required hedge22/7coin times(1+3*0.00035), then rounding up to0.01coin, gives3.15coin. At1x the correct reciprocal hedge is53/22coin;250/arithmeticVWAP would be wrong. With one top bid of250at110, both full/top profiles instead use the same2.51coin reference, proving profile comparability when both can support the fixed face.

## Economic checks and limits

For fixed q and USD face, each1x/2x/3x cell stresses observed ENTRY spot/future crossing and assumed entry/delivery/spot-exit fee rates. Actual terminal spot/TWAP ratio is an exogenous conditional world, not a forecast or an observed future quote. The ratio already includes future spot executable-price, timing and FX differences; it is not a separately estimated exit spread that the implementation scales. Therefore label these entry-crossing/fee stress cells at a fixed terminal-ratio world, not full future round-trip spread stress.

Literal Fraction calculations checked each tier's stressed fills, coin hedge/fees, initial USDT spend, remaining idle capital, terminal coin and actual-exit-ratio conversion, net/10000, reverse break-even ratio and the same-q passive control. Cash plus terminal coins are counted once in this formula. The separate source/native-account bridge must still prove its ownership earmark; this review does not certify it. Passive control shares capital and purchased coins but not realized risk; matched-risk flag remains false. Unknown delivery/exit inputs remain None, not zero or forced convergence. Same reserve coins across fee tiers create explicit residual coin delta and price dependence.
