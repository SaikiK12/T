## Algebra checked independently

Let signed USD face L be positive for long, negative for short. The component's assumed inverse coin PnL for one interval is L*(1/F_previous-1/F_next). A short therefore gains coin when price falls and loses coin when price rises. Same-direction opening fills require weighted reciprocal entry, sum(L_i/F_i)/sum(L_i), not an arithmetic entry average. Daily realization resets the reciprocal checkpoint while keeping face and coin cash. Its increments telescope exactly through final delivery, without reposting cumulative since-entry PnL or adding fictitious USD principal. Adding another fill after a daily mark correctly weights the reset old checkpoint with the new fill reciprocal.

For shorts of positive face A_i, hedge coin is h=sum(A_i/F_i). With initial retained coin C0, entry coin fees E, delivery-index price X and delivery fee fraction d, final coin is A*(1-d)/X+(C0-E-h), where A=sum(A_i). Actual spot exit price is S, not necessarily X. Net quote after exit fee fraction f is [A*(1-d)+(C0-E-h)*X]*(S/X)*(1-f). USDT/USD effects belong to S/X as well. Matching h only removes underlying sensitivity in the ideal index-reference valuation when residual coin is zero; it does not eliminate TWAP-versus-actual-exit risk, owner fees, rounding, counterparty, transfer/session/partial-fill or margin risks. Excess fee/collateral reserve coin leaves explicit residual delta.

Opening admission must compare abs(total new face)/new price to coin cash PLUS old-position unrealized coin PnL minus the supplied new coin fee. Cash alone can falsely admit a new short after adverse price movement or falsely reject a long after an unrealized gain. The parent independently identified this and fixed it before the reviewed hash. Additional review findings were: unknown past fees must block further opening risk instead of using an upper-bound cash component as certified collateral; and Decimal scale must be bounded before Fraction conversion to prevent constructing enormous denominators. Both are addressed in the reviewed hash. A first unknown-fee opening is labelled unknown_fee, leaves cash withheld, and cannot serve as known collateral for additional risk.
