## Question and scope

On the frozen Binance USDⓈ-M point-in-time crypto top-50, does the fixed Kronos-small forecast of each coin's next 24-hour return produce a positive, cost-adjusted top-minus-bottom quintile portfolio that adds to a fixed trailing-30-day-momentum portfolio?

This is a **Binance historical signal screen**, because that is where the archived point-in-time top-50 and delisted-contract history exist. It does not establish MEXC execution or transfer. The current MEXC forward-cost lane remains the source for MEXC cost observations; any MEXC implementation or transfer test needs its own frozen venue specification. This experiment places no orders.

## Universe and data gate

- **Venue/instrument:** Binance USDⓈ-M linear perpetuals, using the owner's hourly archive. Exclude symbols in the frozen TradFi instrument mapping; do not use spot, inverse contracts, or a current-only listing API.
- **Eligible universe:** use the archive's point-in-time top-50 mask exactly as supplied, including contracts later delisted. Do not rebuild membership using future liquidity or replace a missing/delisted contract with a current survivor. Break equal liquidity ranks by the archive's frozen symbol ordering.
- **Manifest required before freeze:** the owner will record one manifest and SHA-256 covering the hourly OHLCV/quote-volume files, funding events, point-in-time eligibility mask, symbol metadata, and TradFi exclusion list. It must identify all delisted names and the history cut. The Codex checkout has no Binance candle archive, so this hash must come from the runner's data environment. No freeze or scoring without it.
- **Rank determinism required before freeze:** verify that the archived top-50 mask and all liquidity cost tiers use deterministic tie breaks matching a recorded symbol order. The current `lab.panel.liquidity_rank` uses chained default `argsort`, which does not promise that ordering for tied volumes. If the frozen mask or tier ranks cannot be reproduced deterministically from the manifest, stop; do not silently claim the tie rule above is already satisfied.
- **Lifecycle and target-completeness gate:** before scoring, validate that every symbol/date used by either portfolio and the equal-weight-universe diagnostic has a complete 24-hour target path. Every hourly close must exist through the target boundary, unless an official Binance contract-termination record supplies the exact effective time and final settlement/exit price. On a documented termination, close the virtual position at that price, keep the proceeds in cash for the rest of the horizon, and do not substitute a successor ticker or contract. A missing bar without a documented event, an ambiguous contract identity, or a missing terminal record makes the lane `INCONCLUSIVE / LIFECYCLE DATA`; stop the full run rather than dropping that coin-date, treating it as zero, or scoring only surviving names. The preflight may expose record presence and finite-value flags while hiding price values; it must not expose or summarize terminal prices, return magnitudes, or signs before the coverage gate passes.
- **Funding completeness gate:** preserve raw settlement timestamps and per-contract funding records. Reconcile the archive with each instrument's expected settlement events so a genuine no-settlement interval is distinguishable from a missing record; allocate a settlement to the position held immediately before its exact event time. Before the coverage gate passes, expose only event presence/timestamps and expected-vs-observed counts, not funding-rate values or PnL. A missing expected event, duplicate/ambiguous event, or unresolved event-to-position timing makes the lane `INCONCLUSIVE / FUNDING DATA`. Do not rely on `lab.engine.forward_returns` converting missing funding to zero.
- **Inputs:** hourly `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`, and `amount`; map Binance `quote_volume` to `amount`. Use no funding, premium, open-interest, order-flow, technical-indicator, or other external feature as a model input. Do not fill missing candles or prices.
- **History requirement:** a coin-date is usable only when it has 721 consecutive valid hourly bars through the signal bar (`i-720..i`). Kronos receives the most recent 512 bars (`i-511..i`); the extra 720 intervals make the fixed momentum control `close[i] / close[i-720]` available. New listings remain in the eligible denominator but have no score until this requirement is met.
- **Coverage-only pass before sizing:** first generate forecasts and inspect only availability/finite-positive-output flags, not future prices or returns. Coverage passes only if (a) valid matched scores cover at least 90% of all eligible coin-date rows and (b) at least 45 of 50 eligible coins have usable scores on at least 95% of eligible signal dates. On a signal date with fewer than 45 usable scores, target weights are zero for the following 24-hour interval; record zero market PnL for that interval and charge any boundary cost from closing the prior weights. If either coverage condition fails, stop with `INCONCLUSIVE / DATA COVERAGE`; do not relax the history rule, impute, drop the date, or size a partial book. This is data/model-output coverage only, not probability calibration.

## Forecast, signal clock, and portfolio

- **Clock and date key:** one signal per UTC day at signal-close time `T = 00:00 UTC`. Row `i` is the candle with `open_time(i) + 1 hour = T`; its final input is available only after it closes. Use rows `i-511..i` and request forecasts for rows `i+1..i+24`. Key the signal, coverage and return record by `T`, not by the input candle's open date. For this historical label, assume an idealized entry at `close[i]` at `T` and predict `close[i+24]`; do not call `open[i+1]` the entry price or assume it equals `close[i]`. This is an hourly close-to-close boundary proxy, not an executable fill.
- **Score:** for each usable coin, `s_i = log(predicted_close[i+24] / observed_close[i])`. Use only the Kronos predicted `close` at step 24. A non-finite or non-positive terminal forecast is unavailable. The 24 future hourly bars are held once, without overlapping cohorts.
- **Ranks and weights:** on each signal date with at least 45 usable names, let `k=floor(N/5)`. Long the highest `k` scores and short the lowest `k`; equal weights `+1/k` and `-1/k`, respectively (gross exposure 2, signed dollar exposure 0). Break ties by symbol ascending. Each daily record contains market PnL from `close[i]` through `close[i+24]`; target weights at `T` replace the prior weights at that boundary. A name held at the same weight/sign remains continuous and incurs no artificial close/reopen charge. The momentum control uses the same dates, same matched names, same ranking, weights, and rebalance rule.
- **Universe hedge:** apply the repository convention `H_t=-sum_i(w_i)` in the equal-weight basket of eligible crypto contracts at that date, with the basket's actual returns and costs. The equal long and short legs make `H_t` zero by construction in normal operation. This is dollar-neutral; it is **not** beta-neutral. Report beta to the equal-weight eligible-universe return as a diagnostic, without changing weights to improve it.
- **Persistence baseline:** forecast the terminal close as `close[i]` (zero log return). Define realized target `y_i=log(close[i+24]/close[i])` and forecast errors as predicted log return minus `y_i`; report MAE/RMSE for Kronos and zero-return persistence on exactly the same valid coin-date rows. Do not compute raw-price MAE/RMSE across coins. All persistence scores are tied, so it does not define a quintile book and must not be given an arbitrary portfolio ranking.
- **30-day-momentum control:** score `m_i = log(close[i] / close[i-720])`; top-minus-bottom quintiles as above. This uses 720 close-to-close intervals and therefore requires closes at both `i-720` and `i` (721 hourly closes). It is the fixed portfolio comparator. No additional momentum horizon or alternate ranking is allowed.

## Returns, funding, costs, and endpoints

- Realized 24-hour asset returns for signal time `T` span rows `i..i+23`, from the idealized `close[i]` entry at `T` to `close[i+24]` at `T+24h`; include each funding settlement on its actual following bar, charging longs and crediting shorts as the lab does. Treat an event stamped exactly at T as belonging to the positions held immediately before T; the new target weights apply immediately after that boundary. Do not use the target-bar close before the position is entered. `lab.engine.forward_returns` leaves missing-close returns as NaN and converts missing funding to zero, while the generic engine skips NaN asset-return terms; the runner must pass both lifecycle and funding gates and must not rely on those fallback behaviors.
- **Latency limit:** an hourly archive cannot measure inference, network, and order-arrival delay inside the hour. The boundary fill is an idealized earliest-entry proxy, not a fill simulation. Report no executable-edge claim from it; any deployment candidate needs a separate prospective paper lane with measured end-to-end delay and venue-specific costs/fills.
- Use the existing one-way tier matrix from `lab.engine.cost_matrix` as the **research cost proxy**: 6 bp for liquidity ranks 1–20, 8 bp for 21–50, 12 bp for 51–100, and 20 bp thereafter. At each UTC signal boundary T, charge `cost_tier * abs(w_T - w_previous)` once per symbol; unchanged signed weights incur no artificial close/reopen fee. Start from zero weights and charge initial entries at the first signal; at the final target boundary T+24h, flatten the book and charge the terminal exit in that final signal-date record. Charge any closing cost on a coverage-abstention date at that date's boundary. Apply and report **1x, 2x, and 3x**. These are assumed fee-plus-slippage tiers, not measured Binance fills, and are not MEXC costs. Report turnover, gross return, funding, cost, net return, drawdown, and both legs separately.
- **Primary statistic:** paired daily net return difference at 1x, `Delta_t = Kronos_t - MOM30_t`, with one observation per UTC signal date. Use `lab.stats.newey_west_t(Delta, lag=30)`. The prospective result is `CONFIRMED` only if the coverage gate passed, Kronos mean net return is positive at 1x, mean `Delta` is positive, and its preregistered Newey–West t is at least 2.0. Otherwise the result is `NULL` or `INCONCLUSIVE` under the gate above. The 2x/3x results are mandatory and cannot be omitted; positive 3x net return is required before the owner can consider any separate capital-gate review.
- **Fixed diagnostics only:** mean daily RankIC (one Spearman correlation across the same covered, matched cross-section per date, then a Newey–West t with lag 30); log-return MAE/RMSE versus zero-return persistence; equal-weight-universe beta; turnover; long/short contribution; gross, funding, costs, net, total return, and drawdown at all three cost multiples. Also report the paired primary statistic at HAC lags 7, 14, 30 and 60 as fixed dependence-sensitivity diagnostics; only the preregistered lag-30 rule decides. No confidence score, probability, interval calibration, threshold, or sizing rule is inferred from stochastic path spread.
- **Dependence and missing dates:** the date, not coin, is the inference unit. Keep the cross-section together and retain abstention dates as zero book returns. Never treat coin-date rows as independent observations. The daily books do not overlap; if the implementation introduces overlap, stop and amend under a new lane before reading outcomes.

3. **Prospective confirmation:** regardless of the historical return sign, first collect 30 consecutive on-time UTC signal dates as a coverage-only warm-up. Do not size a book or access their future returns. Proceed only if the coverage gate passes. Then collect 365 consecutive on-time UTC signal dates; before freeze, record the exact warm-up, first scored, and final signal timestamps. During scoring, a signal date below 45 valid forecasts sets target weights to zero for its following 24 hours; any cost to close the prior weights is included at that boundary. At the end, the same overall coverage thresholds must still pass. Write daily data append-only; monitoring may check pipeline health and coverage but may not inspect performance summaries or retune. The final decision is made once, on the fixed end date. No optional stopping or replacing missed days.

Any change to weights, dates, universe, latency, costs, horizon, baseline, seed, forecast parameters, or position rule requires a new registered round.
