## GEX is closed, twice, with a measured reason

This is not an opinion; it is in the registry:

| lane | what it tested | result |
|---|---|---|
| **R292** `dealerflow_v1` | crypto dealer GEX from 41M Deribit taker-signed trades | **NULL.** Median net GEX -$20M per 1% = **0.13% of Binance perp ADV**. The hedge is too small to move price. Dealers short gamma 76-92% of days. |
| **R416** `gexfilter_v1` | free SqueezeMetrics SPX GEX as a BTC/ETH vol filter | **NULL.** Adds **<=0.003** to R² (t -0.78 / -1.30). |
| R289 / R296 | expiry pinning, settlement window, charm, delta unwind | NULL — too small, too rare |
| R307 | 25-delta risk reversal timing | NULL (24 trials) |
| R209 / R138 | DVOL variance risk premium | NULL |

The mechanism fails for a *quantity* reason, not a modelling reason: in equity index options the
dealer hedge is large relative to the underlying, and in crypto it is ~0.1% of it. Re-running GEX
with a better estimator cannot fix a factor of ~700. The Deribit API is reachable keyless (1064 live
BTC options at the time of writing), so this is not a data-access problem either.

## The structural finding: it is a cost problem, not a signal problem

Both the strategist and the coordinator's own reading of ~51 nulls agree: almost every failure is a
**real gross effect of 1-15 bp/day against 9-19 bp/day of cost**. The lab has been hunting direction
in the most efficient instrument at the highest cost tier.

The obvious escape — rest passive and collect the spread instead of paying it — is also closed:
**R193/R194 NULL on adverse selection**. You get filled precisely when you are wrong. Web maker is
0 bp on 1106/1200 contracts, but a 0 bp fee is not a 0 bp cost.

## What actually survives, and why it is different

The unlock short is the only candidate that clears cost by an order of magnitude:
**+324 bp per event against ~18.7 bp round trip** (2 x 9.3635). It is not a faster signal; it is a
**multi-day hold over a scheduled supply event**, which amortises cost to noise. That horizon change
is what the other fifty ideas never made.

New robustness computed this session, answering the referee's clustering objection:

| clustering | clusters | 95% CI of mean net/event | P(mean<=0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| week | 170 | +1.19% .. +5.18% | 0.0018 |
| month | 58 | +1.34% .. +5.17% | 0.0008 |
| quarter | 22 | +1.07% .. +5.14% | 0.0020 |
| **per token** | 69 | +1.15% .. +5.53% | 0.0008 |

The edge survives **every** dependence structure, including per-token, which is the harshest. Plus
sign test p=1.6e-8 and a symmetric trimmed mean of +4.06% on **all 422 events, no filter**.

### One referee claim rejected, with proof
The referee asserted the trimmed mean was "exactly tail-driven" and the p-values "void for the
filtered strategy". **No filter was applied.** `experiments/unlockrisk_v1/results/report.json`
records `adv_filter: false`, and the trimmed mean drops the top *and* bottom 10% symmetrically — it
is a robustness statistic, not an outcome screen. The ADV>=0.82 tail filter was explicitly rejected
earlier (Fisher p=0.101). A test now pins this (`test_no_outcome_filter_was_applied...`).

## The one test that would change the answer

R265's own FINDINGS names it: the calendar is **today's snapshot, not point-in-time**. Until an
event is knowable *before* the D-7 entry, the +3.24% could partly be hindsight about which unlocks
were recorded.

**Checked this session:** DefiLlama's emissions dataset (`documentedData`, `metadata.unlockEvents`)
carries unlock timestamps but **no publication/announcement timestamp**. So a true PIT calendar is
not obtainable from the held source. It needs either:
1. a source that publishes schedules with a datable announcement (token vesting contracts, project
   docs, exchange notices), or
2. **R266's on-time forward tickets**, which are PIT by construction and already running.

Option 2 is the honest path and it is already in motion. Nothing in this session touches it.

## Bottom line

Under these constraints the reachable edge is the **scheduled-supply event family**, held for days,
sized small (R494: 4.22%/yr, max DD -4.62% at the owner's 2% cap). GEX is not a path. More perp
indicators at API taker are not a path. The decisive next evidence is **R266's forward tickets**,
and the decisive next *new* search is a **frozen reserved-sample batch**, not another look at spent
data.
