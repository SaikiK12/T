## Existing combinations that constrain this work

These close their tested rules and samples, not every interaction. R285 remains an unchanged frozen prospective carry/flow/events blend. Its archive-volatility scaling and event exit-day booking do not establish daily marked capital feasibility or incremental value over parents.

R304's historical interaction score is on already-read dates. R305's severe short squeeze tail and missing-metrics audit matter before adding features. Combining R355 and R364 would concentrate settlement exposure and inherit unresolved cash/missing-record assumptions; their labels do not create independent alpha.

## Required comparison contract

Freeze each hybrid and its full candidate family before any new statistic. Use parent-only, component-only where meaningful, cash-substitution and risk-matched controls on identical dates. Primary evidence is paired incremental net account return versus a benchmark fixed before evaluation; report absolute net PnL as well. Preserve zero-trade days, explicit missing/late eligibility, open-position marks, quantities and per-venue collateral. Charge incremental turnover once, settled funding once, and all execution at 1x/2x/3x costs. Cluster inference by joint calendar/event blocks; correct across declared candidates and disclose prior family selection. A lower-risk portfolio alone does not prove a new signal.

This checkout's data root contains no market datasets. R304's archived aggregates lack instrument holdings and capital paths; R371 has no prospective tickets here; R355/R364 have no scored hops. Thus no honest new executable hybrid PnL can be calculated from the available artifacts. The next experiment needs restored/reconciled point-in-time inputs or new verified captures, not arithmetic on summary means. No new lane ID is reserved by this document.
