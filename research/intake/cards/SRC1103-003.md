The lens agent read and probed them on 2026-11-03, and an independent verifier re-fetched each.

## 1. What the source says

**Exchange rules.**
- No leaderboard request type in the info endpoint.
- Fills:
  - userFills returns at most 2000 most recent fills;
  - userFillsByTime returns at most 2000 per response, and only the 10,000 most recent are available.
- Rate limits:
  - aggregated weight 1200 per minute;
  - weight 2 for clearinghouseState and similar, 20 for other requests, 60 for userRole;
  - at most 10 unique users in websocket user subscriptions.
- Vault terms (legacy):
  - 10% leader profit share; the leader keeps at least 5%;
  - lockups of 4 days (HLP) and 1 day (user vaults);
  - creating a vault costs a 10k USDC fee.

**Probes.**
- HLP's portfolio allTime has 102 points from 2023-05-10, one about every 12-13 days.
- stats-data:
  - vaults is 14.2 MB, with isClosed and createTimeMillis (87 of the first 133 entries closed);
  - leaderboard is 39.6 MB (ethAddress, accountValue, day/week/month/allTime pnl, roi, vlm), updated about hourly.
