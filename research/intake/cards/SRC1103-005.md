Sources:

An independent verifier re-fetched each.

## 1. What the source says

**Exchange rule.**
- pnl and trades by account need no key.
- pnl returns daily points: account 1483 has 599 days from 2025-03-16.
- trades are cursor-paged and carry the taker's and maker's position before each fill.
- Standard accounts get 60 requests per rolling minute (Premium 24,000 and Builder 240,000 weighted).
- Weights: trades 200, recentTrades 600, others 300. Limits apply per IP and per L1 address.

**Published result (Barber et al. 2014, Taiwan day traders 1992-2006).**
- The top 500 by prior-year performance earn 61.3 bp/day before fees and 37.9 after.
- Fewer than 1% earn predictably net of fees.

**Not confirmed:**
- whether pnl values are cumulative;
- the undeclared pnlleaderboard endpoint;
- Lighter's terms of use.

## 2. What we can reproduce

Per-account daily PnL and fills for any account index we sample. There is no public account listing, so a sample must
be drawn by index.

## 3. The experiment it earns, or none

**M467: Lighter winner persistence and mirror.**
- Mechanism: skill persistence among Lighter traders, net of copy delay.
- Inputs: pnl for a random sample of account indices, and trades for the chosen accounts. About 1 GB for 5,000
  histories at 60 requests a minute: owner's yes needed, or a thinner sample.
- Decision: rank on 60-day trade PnL over equity known at t; copy from t + 1 day.
- Falsification:
  - the top decile does not beat the median over the next 30 days;
  - the mirror nets ≤ 0 at 1x/2x/3x;
  - the result comes only from market makers.
