# Research card: SRC1103-010 — Binance forced switch to hourly funding

Source: Binance announcement [Important Updates on Funding Rate Settlement Frequency of USDⓈ-M Perpetual Contracts (2025-05-02)](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/3243c81a35bd4f0c86a37315c3dc96cc). Lens agent read it on 2026-11-03, and an independent verifier re-fetched it. Archive event counts come from the lab's own `data/fundingRate` (interval and rate only; no prices or returns were read).

## 1. What the source says

- **Exchange rule.** Published 2025-04-25 12:30, effective 2025-05-02 08:00 UTC:
  - Trigger: "If the previous funding rate settlement … reaches the funding rate cap/floor", the contract moves to
    hourly settlement.
  - The switch is completed "within approximately 15 minutes".
  - It "will not be reverted to every eight hours or every four hours". Any reversal comes by separate
    announcement.
  - The per-interval cap is unchanged (±0.3% in the BTC example).
- **Lab archive counts (verifier re-computed every number).** Since 2025-05-02:
  - 292 drops to 1 h (191 from 4 h, 101 from 8 h) on 214 symbols.
  - 220 have |trigger rate| ≥ 0.3%, 218 of them exactly at ±2% (214 at −2%).
  - 58 have rate 0 and 14 have 0 < |rate| < 0.3%.
  - All 220 cap-triggered switches settle again 1 h after the print. The other 72 have gaps of 3, 4 or 6 h and land
    at fixed hours (66 at 20:00 UTC): manual or announced.
- **Before the rule:** 303 prints ≥ 1.99% on 102 symbols (2022-10-03..2025-04-29), and 1 drop to 1 h.
- **Contradiction inside the source set:** despite "will not be reverted", the archive shows 217 changes 1 h → 4 h and
  69 changes 1 h → 8 h after 2025-05-02, made by announcement. The hourly state is not permanent.

## 2. What we can reproduce

- The announcement's rule and dates, and the archive counts (switch events, intervals, trigger rates).
- **Not read:** any price, return, or Lighter minute around these settlements.
- **Lab scope check.** R353's PREREG keeps settlements 4-24 h after the previous one, and R354's keeps 4 h and 8 h
  intervals only. R356, R362 and R363 decide R354's hops. So no settlement lane has read a 1 h-interval Binance
  settlement.

## 3. The experiment it earns, or none

**M460: hourly-cycle settlement scalp (R363's rule on 1 h-interval Binance settlements).**
- Mechanism: the capture crowd's exit after each deep hourly lump, as R363 found on 4 h and 8 h lumps.
- Observable inputs: Binance settled funding with the interval taken from the gap (held); Lighter funding (held);
  Lighter 1-minute candles around each hop (public, fetchable).
- Causal decision timestamp: before T, from rates known at the decision, as R363.
- Falsification: on the 1 h-interval hops, CR1 t of the 1x net < 2, or the 2x mean ≤ 0, or any of R363's other
  checks fails.

**M461: forced-switch natural experiment.**
- Mechanism: post-rule floor prints (switched) against pre-rule ones (not switched).
- Observable inputs: `data/fundingRate`, `data/klines_1h` (held).
- Causal decision timestamp: entry after T + 15 min.
- Falsification: post-minus-pre ≤ 0 or t < 2, or 2x net ≤ 0.
- Seen data overlap: R273 and M261 read negative-funding onsets, so this is gate 0 only.
