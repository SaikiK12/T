## 1. What the source says

- **Exchange rule.** BRENTOIL and GASOIL indices roll from the front to the next futures month in five steps of 20%,
  on business days 5-9, at 23:59 London.
- **The table and the prose disagree by about a minute.** The November BRENTOIL row reads "2026-11-06 7:00PM to
  2026-11-12 7:00PM ET", F27 → G27.
  - The table is "reference only".

- **Exchange rule (funding).**
  - A premium sample at a random second each minute; TWAP of 60.
  - Small clamp 0.05%, big clamp 4%/8 h (0.5%/h), divided by 8, interest 0.01%/8 h.
  - Settles hourly.
- **Lens only, not verifier-checked:** trade.xyz rolls on business days 5-9 or 5-10, with NATGAS steps at 5:30 pm ET;
  its docs contradict each other.

## 3. The experiment it earns, or none

**M462: index roll-step lag.**
- Mechanism: the index jumps by 0.2 × (next − front) at each step. If the mark follows the index while the book
  lags, a position in the step direction from step − 30 min to step + 30 min collects it.
- Observable inputs: Lighter index or oracle and book around each step (record forward); the front and next futures
  spread (public settlement prices).
- Causal decision timestamp: step − 30 min, from the published schedule and the last known spread.
- Falsification: the mean step-direction return net of 1x cost ≤ 0 over the first 20 steps, or the book moves before
  the step.
- Power: five steps a month on one or two contracts, so this is an event study for months.
