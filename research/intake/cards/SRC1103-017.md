# Research card: SRC1103-017 — Korean FSC: "racehorse" and "cage pumping"

Source: Financial Services Commission press release of 2025-04-17, [fsc.go.kr/no010101/84387](https://fsc.go.kr/no010101/84387) (Korean), and its etoday report. An independent verifier re-fetched the release. The live public APIs of Upbit and Bithumb were probed (SRC1103-018..020).

## 1. What the source says

- **Regulator release (allegations in referred cases, not a court finding).**
  - **"Racehorse" (경주마):** heavy buying around the hour when the daily price-change rate resets, then orders 1-2 a
    second concentrated over 20-30 minutes. The reset hour "differs by exchange (00:00, 09:00, 11:00, etc.)".
  - **"Cage pumping" (가두리):** when a trading-caution designation stops deposits and withdrawals, arbitrage is
    impossible. Small and mid coins with thin float are then pumped to "up to 10x+ above other exchanges", and crash
    back to the prior price.
- The FSC names no exchange.

## 2. What we can reproduce

- **Upbit's reset is 00:00 UTC (09:00 KST).** This is our check, not the FSC's: the KRW-BTC ticker's opening_price
  equals the day candle that opened at 2026-11-03T00:00 UTC.
- **Upbit hourly candles** for KRW-XRP reach back to at least 2022-12-23. The earliest date was not checked.
- **Upbit `market/all?is_details=true`:**
  - 875 markets, each with market_event {warning, caution: 5 flags}.
  - At about 09:07 UTC, 15 of 299 KRW markets had a caution flag.
  - It shows the current state only.
- **Bithumb `assetsstatus/ALL`:** 60 of 523 assets had deposits or withdrawals off. Current state only.
- **Not read:** any price or return.

## 3. The experiment it earns, or none

**M464: reset-hour reversal.**
- Mechanism: the 00:00-01:00 UTC Upbit excess return over Binance reverts.
- Inputs: Upbit hourly candles (to fetch; size to the owner first) and Binance hourly bars (held).
- Decision: at 01:00 UTC.
- Falsification: no reversal on the global perp over 6 or 24 h net of 1x cost, or no difference from the 02-23 UTC
  placebo hours.

**M465: cage-pump fade.**
- Mechanism: the collapse after a flagged pump.
- Inputs: Upbit hourly candles, plus Upbit market_event and Bithumb wallet status recorded forward (no history).
- Decision: the first Upbit hourly close with premium > 20% and volume > 5x its median.
- Falsification: the global perp does not move during the pump (NULL), or the short nets ≤ 0 at 1x/2x/3x.
