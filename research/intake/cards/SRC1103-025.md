# Research card: SRC1103-025 — Ex-dividend settlement on equity perps

Sources:
- Seo et al., [Basis-Only Funding Is Not Dividend Fair](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22839), arXiv 2610.22839v1 (2026-10-16, MARBLE 2026).
- The Bitget dividend notice of 2026-06-12 ([support article 12560603885597](https://www.bitgetapp.com/support/articles/12560603885597)).

The lens agent read §6-7 of the paper through a tool summary. An independent verifier re-fetched the abstract, §6 and Table 1.

## 1. What the source says

**Working paper (Seo et al.):**
- Binance posts a single funding entry of −D/(Mark−D), within 5e-6 of the dividend, at ex − 1 20:00 ET.
- Hyperliquid and OKX standard legs post none.
- Bitget settles by a snapshot credit outside the funding tape.
- **Sample:** 12 ex-dates over 7 names; Table 1 has only 5 Binance ex-dates:

  | Name | Ex-date | Size |
  |---|---|---|
  | AAPL | 05-11 | 9.2 bp |
  | MSFT | 05-21 | 21.8 bp |
  | NVDA | 06-04 | 11.7 bp |
  | GOOGL | 06-08 | 6.0 bp |
  | META | 06-15 | 9.1 bp |

- The authors call it "settlement-leg size, not an arbitrage net of fees", and their evidence "low-powered" and
  "qualitative".
- Premium AR(1) 0.72, half-life about 2.1 h.

**Exchange rule (Bitget):**
- Longs credited and shorts debited "through funding fee adjustments".
- On a position snapshot at 2026-06-13 08:00 UTC+8, two days before ex.
- Netted per user ID.

**Hyperliquid HIP-3 docs:** silent on dividends; the deployer defines the contract.

**Not found:**
- The Binance FAQ (bot challenge, not bypassed).
- The MEXC dividend notice (403). A snippet says longs are credited and shorts debited from a 01:00 UTC snapshot.

## 2. What we can reproduce

The lens agent counted atoms in held data (no P&L):

- In MEXC TradFi funding:
  - 51 of 67 matched events from 2026-06-11 show an atom-like row within 20% of −D/(Mark−D);
  - 16 do not, and their symbol mapping is unverified.
- Median D/P is 24.8 bp.

No venue fees, French access to equity perps, or returns were checked.

## 3. The experiment it earns, or none

**M463: ex-dividend settlement mismatch.**
- Mechanism: on a non-crediting venue a long bears the ex-open drop D; on a crediting venue it is paid D. Long the
  crediting venue, short the non-crediting one across the ex-date.
- Inputs:
  - held: Binance atoms, MEXC funding and klines;
  - to record: Hyperliquid HIP-3, Gate, KuCoin and Lighter equity-perp funding, and a dividend calendar.
- Decision: the last settlement at least 1 h before the US close on ex − 1, on declared dividends only.
- Falsification:
  1. Audit first, 0 trials: if every lab-tradable venue credits D, the trade does not exist (NULL).
  2. Then the mean pair net ≤ 0 at 1x over at least 30 events, or the non-crediting premium absorbs D before ex.

**Side finding:** a Binance or MEXC stock-perp short held across an ex-date pays the dividend through funding.
