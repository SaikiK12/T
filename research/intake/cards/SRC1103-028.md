# Research card: SRC1103-028 — Prediction markets are not legal for the owner

Sources:
- ANJ notice, [blocage du site Polymarket](https://anj.fr/promotion-dune-offre-de-jeux-dargent-illegale-blocage-du-site-polymarket) (published 2026-07-17).
- The [Kalshi member agreement](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/kalshi-member-agreement.pdf).
- Two papers: [arXiv 2607.26245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26245) (OpenMarket) and
  [arXiv 2609.12878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12878).

An independent verifier re-fetched each on 2026-11-03.

## 1. What the source says

**Regulator decision (ANJ).**
- On 2026-07-16 the ANJ ordered French ISPs to block Polymarket.
- Grounds:
  - after a formal notice the operator geoblocked, but this was circumvented in practice;
  - 578,751 French visits in June.
- Advertising an unauthorised site risks a €100k fine.
- In February 2026 the ANJ reminded that prediction sites are not authorised in France.

**Contract (Kalshi).** France is a "Restricted Jurisdiction", with the UK, Poland and Monaco.

**Working papers.**
- **OpenMarket:** the model "does not beat … the probability already implied by Polymarket's own order book",
  −0.116 payoff units a trade; Polymarket reacts to Binance in a median 347 ms.
- **2609.12878:** 588M trades.
  - Contracts under 10c lose 19.3c per dollar; at or above 90c they earn +0.83c.
  - There is no effect in sports.
