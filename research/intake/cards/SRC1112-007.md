# Research card: SRC1112-007 Ethena docs: sUSDe mechanics, revenue, risks

Source: seven full pages of [Ethena's docs](https://docs.ethena.fi/technical-design/staking-usde), read on 2026-11-12
through the `.md` routes, with charts not viewed. Pages: Staking USDe, Rewards Mechanism, Staking Key Functions,
Reserve Fund, Protocol Revenue, Funding Risk, Exchange Failure Risk. The EEA and aggregator statements were
re-fetched by web_fetch on the HTML routes: VERIFIED.

## 1. What the source says
- **Product rules:**
  - sUSDe is an ERC-4626 vault on USDe, and its USDe value only rises or stays flat.
  - Rewards are paid every 8 hours and vest linearly over 8 hours.
  - Rewards relate to the previous week.
  - The page warns that "the most recent 8 hourly payment ... as seen on some data aggregator sites" differs
    from the published APY.
  - Cooldown on unstaking can be set up to 90 days. DefiLlama's poolMeta says 7 days.
  - Sanctioned addresses can be frozen.
  - "The acquisition of sUSDe is not offered to persons with their habitual residence or registered office in the
    European Union or the European Economic Area."
- **Revenue (issuer statements):**
  - Revenue comes from funding and basis on delta-neutral trades, lending, tokenised RWA yield and stablecoin
    rewards.
  - The Reserve Fund bears negative revenue, and its share of revenue is currently 0%.
  - Funding Risk states BTC/ETH funding averaged 7.8-9%/yr over three years and that the longest negative
    streak was 13 days. These are the issuer's numbers, not checked here.
- **Exchange failure (issuer statement):** backing sits with off-exchange settlement providers, so exposure to a
  failed exchange is limited to P&L between daily settlement cycles.
