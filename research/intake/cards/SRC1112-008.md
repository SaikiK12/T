# Research card: SRC1112-008 He, Manela, Ross, von Wachter, Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures

Source: [arXiv 2212.06888v7](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.06888v7) (v7, 2026-09-17), HTML read on 2026-11-12.
- Read: abstract, section 1, 4.1, 4.3 (text and table captions) and 4.4 (text, Table 8, drawdown paragraph).
- Not read: proofs in sections 2-3, sections 5-6 and the appendices.

## 1. What the source says
- **Mechanism (working-paper result):**
  - No-arbitrage perp price with Binance's funding clamp.
  - Trade: short perp and long spot when the deviation rho > 0 (the reverse when rho < 0), and close when rho
    returns to 0.
- **Market and data:** Binance hourly perp and spot for BTC, ETH, BNB, DOGE and ADA, from 2020 to 2024-03-11.
- **Costs (Table 8):**
  - Fees per side: spot 0, 2.25, 4.5 and 6.75 bp; perp 0, 0.18, 0.72 and 1.44 bp.
  - The spread-adjusted variant also pays half the daily effective spread (Roll-type estimate from Binance
    aggTrades).
  - High tier with spread: BTC Sharpe 3.27 and 12.55%/yr; ETH 4.45 and 18.18%/yr; max drawdowns 2.63% and 2.65%.
  - Sharpe and returns are annualised over active hours.
- **Decomposition (Table 7, text only):** price convergence dominates, and funding payments are a minor and
  shrinking share. Deviations fall about 22 percentage points a year.
- **Caveats the authors state:** liquidation also depends on leverage, maintenance margin, collateral and the
  margining regime. Drawdowns reach 13.90% for DOGE.
