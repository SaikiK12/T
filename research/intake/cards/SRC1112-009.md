# Research card: SRC1112-009 Schmeling, Schrimpf, Todorov, Crypto carry (BIS WP 1087)

## 1. What the source says
- **Published working-paper result:**
  - Crypto carry (futures minus spot) averages above 10%/yr and reaches about 60%/yr.
  - A CME 1-month BTC cash-and-carry has an annualised Sharpe of about 0.6 before costs and financing spreads.
- **Risk mechanism:**
  - The basis can widen before maturity, which triggers margin calls on the short futures leg.
  - With no cross-margining (CME), the futures leg at 10x leverage "would have been liquidated in more than half
    of the months".
  - A 10% rise in carry predicts a 44% rise in sell (short) liquidations relative to open interest. Long
    liquidations are not predicted.
  - As of 2021 FTX capped the loss on a futures position at USD 30,000, so a $1m short was liquidated after a 3%
    BTC rise.
- **Caveats:** the sample covers dated futures (CME and others) to 2022, not perpetual funding.
