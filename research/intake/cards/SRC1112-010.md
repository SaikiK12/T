# Research card: SRC1112-010 Campbell, Hey, Moallemi, Nutz, Risk-Based Auto-Deleveraging

Source: [arXiv 2603.15963v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.15963v2) (2026-07-16), HTML read on 2026-11-12.
- Read: abstract, section 4 introduction, and 4.1 "The ADL Event" and "Data sources".
- Not read: proofs, sections 2-3 detail, 4.2-4.3 results and the appendices.
- Read with it: [Hyperliquid's ADL page](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/auto-deleveraging)
  (SRC1112-011, full).

## 1. What the source says
- **Exchange rule (Hyperliquid):**
  - When an account goes negative, opposite-side users are ranked by `(mark_price / entry_price) *
    (notional_position / account_value)`.
  - They are closed at the previous mark price against the underwater account.
- **Working-paper result:** the minimax-leverage ("water-filling") ADL is optimal in the isolated-margin setting.
  In cross-margin it applies to factor-adjusted leverage, so better-hedged portfolios are delevered less.
- **The event (empirical):**
  - On 2025-10-10 Hyperliquid's realized ADL notional was $2.047bn on a 75-coin universe.
  - It came in bursts within 21:16-21:21 UTC, in waves of $176.9m (HYPE, PUMP, FARTCOIN), $93.3m and $386.6m
    (ETH, SOL, BTC, XRP).
  - Intraday, BTC, ETH and SOL fell about 10-20% and thinner coins more than 50%.
- **Data:** public Hyperliquid REST trades, plus Allium account snapshots (a vendor) from 2025-09-22.

## 2. What we can reproduce
- Exposure: committed trades_v2 shows every audited carry book held 14-21 pairs on 2025-10-10. For example,
  R278 A had 19 of 21 pairs short on Hyperliquid.
- Not observable keyless:
  - which accounts were ADL'd (per-user fills are limited, SRC1103-001);
  - Allium snapshots;
  - Hyperliquid 1-minute candles for October 2025 (the candle endpoint keeps recent bars only, not checked here).

## 3. The experiment it earns, or none
- **Mechanism:** a crash triggers ADL of the books' profitable legs, which leaves the other legs naked.
- **Inputs:** pairs open from committed trades_v2, plus public daily high/low candles of the long-leg venue.
- **Causal timestamp:** the ADL window 21:16-21:21 UTC on 2025-10-10. The naked leg is marked to the day's worst
  print (a bound).
- **Falsification:** the bound is below one month of the book's 2x net.
- **Queue:** M534; no lane yet. Trials added: one per book once registered (not yet).
