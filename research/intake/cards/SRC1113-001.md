## 1. What the source says
- **Exchange rule (LLP access):** LLP "is now exclusively accessible to LIT stakers"; "For every 1 LIT staked,
  participants may deposit up to 10 USDC into the LLP". Unstaking has a 3-day lock-up; LLP withdrawal has a 5-minute
  cooldown and no lock-up.
- **Exchange rule (staking):** a "fixed 6% APR"; reward LIT "is bought from
  `0xADbE1265C0df1B623A56d324De494Bcb8e8914Ff`". LIT Fee Credits proceeds go to stakers, streamed daily.
- **Exchange rule (buybacks):** "LIT is bought back by the protocol using trading fee revenue. Buybacks are executed
  via daily 24-hour TWAPs, with the flexibility to use shorter timeframes depending on market conditions." The
  treasury account is `0x000...000`. No size, burn or schedule is stated.
- **Exchange rule (LLP risk):** partial liquidations pay "up to 1% liquidation fee" to LLP. In full liquidation LLP
  takes over positions in ascending unrealized PnL while staying above its initial margin; the rest go to ADL. LLP
  strategies (crypto, FX, equities/RWA) isolate losses per bucket.
- **Exchange rule (fees):** Standard accounts 0/0 on regular orders; Premium 0.40/2.80 bp maker/taker at 0 LIT
  staked, 0.28/1.96 bp at 500,000 LIT.
