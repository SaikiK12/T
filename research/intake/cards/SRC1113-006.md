## 1. What the source says
- **Exchange data:** one point a day at 00:00 UTC. buyback and buyback_usdc start 2025-12-30; the others 2025-01-17.
  `period=d` returns 400 for buyback. Units are not documented; read as LIT and USDC for buyback, USD otherwise
  (UNVERIFIED).
- **Measured here (description, not a test):**
  - 19,729,557 LIT bought for $34,854,124 to 2026-11-10 (average $1.767; 7.89% of 250m circulating).
  - Monthly buyback USDC is within 1.8% of maker plus taker fees from February to September 2026; 114% in January,
    137% in October.
  - Fees fell from $13.74m (2025-11) to $2.1m-$3.0m a month in 2026.
  - Largest liquidation day 2025-10-10: $377.2m volume, $1.43m fee.

## 2. What we can reproduce
- Daily buyback and fee series: yes, keyless, about 316 days of buybacks.
- LIT perp and spot daily candles and hourly funding: `lit/` on `claude/okx-xperp-data` (R447).
- Not observable: intraday TWAP timing, whether bought LIT is burned or held, the staking reward flow.

## 3. The experiment it earns, or none
- Mechanism: a fee-funded daily TWAP is a predictable bid; larger buyback relative to turnover may lift the next
  days' LIT return, unless the market prices the public series.
- Observable inputs: buyback_usdc by day (published after the day), LIT perp and spot daily quote volume and closes.
- Causal decision timestamp: the buyback for day D is known only after D closes (00:00 UTC D+1); the trade opens
  after that, on Lighter's LIT perp.
- Falsification: no rank correlation between the buyback share of turnover and the next week's LIT return, against
  a circular-shift null, at 1x/2x/3x costs.
