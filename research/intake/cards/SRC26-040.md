## 1. What the source says

**Published result.** Deribit BTC options activity has a settlement-related morning peak and a separate U.S.-market-hours peak. The minute-level discussion reports activity spiking at 08:00 and declining rapidly within approximately five minutes. This is trading-activity evidence, not a demonstrated BTC-direction or net-return strategy.

No executable fee, queue, fill or latency model was verified in the received scope.
