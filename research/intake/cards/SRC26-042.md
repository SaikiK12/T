## 1. What the source says

The reel displays chart examples, a parameter-optimization slide and TradingView historical strategy reports. The public script describes a 09:30–09:45 New York opening range and close-confirmed breaks. September release notes change default risk and minimum target-distance filtering. Today's script therefore cannot be silently treated as the June reel's frozen implementation. The product's performance claims are author claims; TradingView simulation is not independent brokerage verification.

## 2. What we can reproduce

The range and signal description can seed an independent rule. Stops, targets, filters, sizing, exact revision and all tester properties still block a replication. Do not transfer MNQ/CME results to MEXC perps without contract/basis/cost evidence.
