## 1. What the source says

The principal rule enters at the second five-minute candle's open in the first candle's direction, skipping a doji. It does not wait for that candle's high/low to break. The opposite first-candle extreme is the stop; the target is 10R or the close. Sizing risks 1% subject to a 4x notional cap. The simulation charges a per-share commission and assumes no fill slippage; the authors later discuss that limitation.
