## 1. What the source says

It forms a 09:30–10:00 New York range and average volume per opening-range bar. It marks the first qualifying long signal before 11:30: close above the range high and VWAP, volume at least 1.5 times the opening-range average, and close in the bar's upper half. There is no short book, position sizing, stop, exit or cost model. There is no explicit bar-confirmation guard. Chart timeframe/session and alert-close configuration affect the observable signal.

## 2. What we can reproduce

This provides a clearer component definition than unspecified confluence. Reading a source does not validate it. Current-bar volume is available only when that bar finishes; a close-derived signal cannot assume a prior or guaranteed same-close executable fill. VWAP reset must be specified.
