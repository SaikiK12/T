## Deductions that killed candidates (no data read)
1. **Binance USDC passive making with a rebate.**
   - At a 0% fee the maker loses about 0.5 bp a fill: R431 Part C measured +0.55 bp of taker markout at 1 s against a
     0.01-0.04 bp touch.
   - The −0.8 bp tier would leave about +0.27 bp a fill before inventory and cancel losses.
   - That tier needs 1.5% of the venue's maker volume. Application alone needs $100M of volume a month, about 330
     times $10k of capital a day.
   - **Killed on scale.** Entered in the `docs/INFRA_GAP.md` ledger. M292 and M427 stay as they are.
2. **A slow maker behind Lighter's speed bump.**
   - Per `docs/VENUE_STRUCTURE.md`, Standard accounts wait 300 ms on taker orders, cancels and modifies. Premium
     takers wait 140 ms, and Premium makers cancel at 0 ms.
   - A Standard maker therefore cannot pull a stale quote before a Premium taker hits it. The speed bump protects
     Premium makers from Standard takers, not us.
   - A Premium maker needs its cancel to land in under about 140 ms. From this container the one-way delay alone is
     about 125 ms. That is unmeasured and marginal.
   - The native stream is also refused by jurisdiction (R428).
   - **Killed for a Standard account**, with a note on M474. The Premium form stays open only through M474's
     queue-model successor.
3. **Shorting a token unlock 30 days ahead.**

     Tokenomist; its page returned 403, so this is UNVERIFIED.
