# Fee map and cost method, 2026-11-16

**Why.** On 2026-11-16 the owner asked not to conclude that fees kill everything, but to find a method that beats them.
They also brought venues that do not serve French residents (Binance, Gate, KuCoin, MEXC) back into scope. The
owner accepts the access risk, which this note states once in section 7.

**What this note is.** It is a source intake and a ranking of leads. It contains:
- a fee map from official pages;
- one descriptive snapshot of book costs;
- arithmetic on results already published by the lab.

It changes no verdict and decides nothing; the frozen lanes named in section 6 do that.

Files:
- `fee_map_20261116_venues.csv`: the fee map, one row per venue and account type, each with its source and status.
- `fee_map_20261116_books.csv`: the book snapshot of 2026-11-16 16:13 UTC, made by `scripts/snapshot_route_costs.py`.

## 1. The short answer
1. **The fee is one term out of four.** An order costs:
   - the venue's fee;
   - half the spread;
   - the walk through the book;
   - for a resting (maker) order, the adverse move after it fills.

   Zero-fee routes exist and are measured below. On them, the remaining cost is spread and walk.
2. **The cheapest route the lab can reach is Lighter's Standard account** (0 maker, 0 taker, through the API, with a
   300 ms delay on takers).
   - In today's snapshot, a $1k taker order costs, per side, all-in:
     - 0.33 bp on BTC and 0.55 bp on ETH;
     - 0.5-1.2 bp on Lighter's stock, index and gold perps;
     - a median of 2.9 bp over the 41 coins that Lighter, Paradex and Hyperliquid all list.
   - Hyperliquid costs 6.0 bp for the same order, most of it its 4.5 bp taker fee.
3. **Paradex is a second zero-fee route, through the API too.** The lab's notes said otherwise, and are corrected in
   section 4.
   - Its retail book is deep at the top: the cost at $10k is about its cost at $1k.
   - It is unstable: SOL, BNB and TRX moved from 2-5 bp to 48-99 bp a side within ten minutes.
4. **On the non-French venues, fees are not the binding cost; the spread on the long tail is.**
   - C14's Binance + Gate carry book pays 18.4 bp a leg a side at $1k once the venues' fees are added to R495's
     measured walk.
   - That is under its 26.9 bp break-even.
   - At $5k it pays 31.7 bp, which is over break-even. R495 reported $5k as inside break-even because its comparison
     left the fee out.
5. **The method that changes that is execution, not a cheaper fee tier.**
   - Rest a maker order on the wide leg (Gate) and hedge it at once with a taker on the tight leg (Binance).
   - On R495's medians, with an adverse move of 5-15 bp, a leg would cost 8-13 bp a side at $5k instead of 31.7.
   - That would turn C14 from about −0.7%/yr to about +2.0 to +2.7%/yr.
   - This is arithmetic on seen medians, not a measurement. Section 6 says how to measure it.
6. **Cost cannot add gross.** At zero cost, C14 earns about +3.9%/yr on total capital (the intercept of R495's fit to
   C14's published 1x/2x/3x nets), which is about the 13-week bill.
   - The method can make a small edge executable.
   - It cannot make a small edge large.
   - Families whose gross is near zero gain nothing from it.

**Programmes that a small account can use**, and what each is worth:
- **Token discounts** (BNB 10%, HYPE staking 5-40%, LIT staking 2.5-30%) cut only the fee term.
  - At 4.5 bp, 10% is 0.45 bp.
- **Referral codes** are small or one-off.
  - Hyperliquid gives 4% off the first $25M of volume.
  - Binance's Referral Pro share is set by the inviter (0-10%) and lasts 30 days.
  - Bybit and BingX give one-off bonuses.
- **VIP tiers** start at millions of dollars of monthly volume (OKX VIP1: $5M or $100k of assets). A $10k account
  does not reach one.
- **Rebates for makers** exist only for market makers at volume: Hyperliquid, dYdX tier 6-7, OKX VIP7, and Gate's
  market-maker tiers.

The fee programmes therefore give a few tenths of a bp. The routes in section 3 save several bp.

## 4. Corrections to the lab's record
1. **Paradex retail is reachable through the API.**
   - `docs/VENUE_STRUCTURE.md` said "The Paradex retail fee applies to UI orders. Any API strategy pays the Pro
     schedule".
   - Paradex's trader-profiles page says an order is a Retail Order "when it is submitted through the Paradex web
     interface or through the API using an interactive JWT token" (`POST /v1/auth?token_usage=interactive`).
   - The limits are 1,000 orders a day and a 300 ms delay.
   - The live `markets` endpoint lists `interactive_fee` 0/0 for all 65 perps.
   - VENUE_STRUCTURE stays unchanged, because R426 (`maker_pilot_v1`) hashes it as a frozen input. This note is the
     correction, and the methods queue links it.
2. **R495 (PR #100, unmerged) compared walk without fee against C14's all-in 7 bp unit.**
   - Its own PREREG says the fee is reported separately.
   - With Binance's 5 bp and Gate's listed 7.5 bp taker fee added, the pair cost per leg per side is:
     - 18.4 bp at $1k (2.63x, about +1.2%/yr);
     - 31.7 bp at $5k (4.53x, about −0.7%/yr);
     - 48.2 bp at $10k (6.89x, about −3.1%/yr).
   - So C14 is a $1k-per-leg book at taker cost, not a $1k-$5k one.
   - This uses R495's own linear extrapolation of C14's 1x/2x/3x nets, which it flags as an approximation.
   - It is for the integration of PR #100. It is not posted as a review comment.
3. **MEXC's 0/0 contracts are web-only.**
   - API fees override promotions, so the API pays 6/8 bp, and the futures API needs KYC.
   - A book that needs API orders on MEXC pays 8 bp plus the walk. The 0/0 route is for a few orders a day placed by
     hand.

## 5. The method: five levers, in the order they pay
| # | Lever | What it saves | When it works | When it fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Route to the zero-fee venue that lists the instrument** (Lighter; Paradex retail; MEXC 0/0 by hand) | the whole fee: 4.5-8 bp a side | every taker family, if the coin is listed and the walk is small at the size traded | thin books (long-tail alts, Paradex oil and gas); order caps (Paradex 1,000 a day, MEXC web about 15 a day by hand) |
| 2 | **Hedged maker on the wide leg**: rest on the illiquid venue, and take on the liquid one as soon as the rest fills | the wide leg's spread and walk, less the maker fee and the adverse move | slow, hedged books such as cross-venue carry, where a fill a few hours late costs little and the hedge removes the outright move | directional signals: R193/R194 found unhedged maker entries adverse-selected (M153); fills are uncertain, so paper must use trade-through fills |
| 3 | **Smaller size, or slices** | the walk, which grows fast with size on long-tail books (Gate: 17 → 66 bp from $1k to $10k) | slow books that can spread an entry over hours | fast signals |
| 4 | **Trade less**: wider entry/exit bands, fee-aware thresholds (R186), netting books against each other | cost × turnover | books whose gross survives slower trading | R231: partial adjustment of a slow trend gave up more gross than it saved (M036) |
| 5 | **Fee programmes**: token discounts, referral codes, VIP tiers | tenths of a bp | always, if the account holds the token | never decisive at the owner's size |

**Two structural facts behind levers 1 and 2:**
- **Speed bumps protect resting orders.**
  - Lighter delays takers by 300 ms (140 ms on Premium, where cancels and post-only orders are not delayed).
  - Paradex delays retail takers by 300 ms and does not delay Pro cancels.
  - A venue that delays takers more than cancels lets a maker withdraw a stale quote before a fast taker hits it.
  - That is the opposite of the setting in which R193/R194 found maker entries adverse-selected.
  - It is not yet measured here.
- **Retail price improvement.**
  - Paradex's RPI quotes match only retail takers and are hidden from the API book.
  - The interactive book is what a retail taker meets.
  - Whether a retail taker's actual fill matches it after the 300 ms delay is the measurement that matters, and it
    is not done.

## 7. Venue access
On 2026-11-16 the owner put venues that do not serve French residents back into research scope, and said they will
use them without KYC.
- This note neither helps with nor comments on how an account is opened. Real money stays the owner's act under hard
  rules 5 and 6.
- The risk, stated once: a venue that does not serve a country can freeze an account or its withdrawals when it finds
  a user from that country.
- Foreign accounts are declared in France each year.
- Lighter, Paradex, Hyperliquid and Aster are on-chain venues used from a wallet.
