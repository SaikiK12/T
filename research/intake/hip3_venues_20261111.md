# Hyperliquid HIP-3 dexes: fees, funding, listings (intake, 2026-11-11)

Checked by the author on 2026-11-11 from Hyperliquid's public API (`perpDexs`, `metaAndAssetCtxs` with `dex`) and
two documentation pages. This page feeds R451 and R453 (`experiments/flow_hlstock_v1`, `experiments/flow_hlstock_mc_v1`)
and records why two ideas got no lane. It is not evidence of a return.

**Labels:** [A] checked by the author, page or endpoint fetched; [S] search snippet only.

## Fees [A]
- hyperliquid.gitbook.io, trading/fees: one fee tier per user across perps, HIP-3 perps and spot; tier-0 perp taker
  4.5 bp, maker 1.5 bp.
- The page's formula: `scaleIfHip3 = deployerFeeScale < 1 ? deployerFeeScale + 1 : deployerFeeScale * 2`;
  `growthModeScale = growthMode ? 0.1 : 1`.
- "When growth mode is activated for an HIP-3 perp, protocol fees, rebates, volume contributions, and L1 user rate
  limit contributions are reduced by 90%." "Setting growth mode has a 30 day cooldown per asset."
- **xyz on 2026-11-11:** fee scale 1.0 on all 122 live markets, growth mode on 117 (blank on 5, among them
  `xyz:GOLD`). Taker fee: 0.9 bp one way in growth mode, 9 bp without it.
- `scripts/record_xyz_books.py` records these settings daily (`markets/` on `claude/xyz-books-data`).

## Funding [A]
- docs.trade.xyz changelog, 2025-12-19: "Funding Rate XYZ (F) = 0.5 [Average Premium Index (P) + clamp (interest
  rate - Premium Index (P), -0.0005, 0.0005)]"; the update "lowers baseline funding to ~5.5% annualized".
- `perpDexs` on 2026-11-11: xyz's funding multiplier is 0.5 on 136 of 138 listed assets (0.005 on 2); its interest
  rate is overridden to 0 only for EUR, GBP, JPY and KRW.
- Other dexes' settings, as listed: io 0.5 (13 assets) and 0.125 (2); para 0.6 on 29; km a 5%/yr interest rate on
  most assets; flx multiplier 0 on all 15; cash 0.000001.
- **No lane: xyz funding carry** (short the xyz perp, hold the stock at an EU broker). The baseline is about 5.5%/yr
  on notional. With the stock fully paid and the perp at 2x margin, that is about 3.7%/yr on capital before costs,
  under the 4% hurdle. R442 found the same level on OKX's stock X-Perps (funding +3.7%/yr, NULL).

## Listings [A]
- `perpDexs` lists 10 dexes: xyz, flx, vntl, hyna, km, abcd, cash, para, mkts, io.
- **On 2026-11-11, every market of km, flx, vntl, cash, mkts, hyna and abcd is delisted.** Only xyz (122 live),
  io and para have live markets (mkts keeps one, BVIV).
- **Venue risk:** a HIP-3 deployer's markets can end. Any xyz book carries the risk that xyz delists or changes its
  fee or funding settings; the daily markets file dates such changes.
- **Live underlyings on two dexes:** 20, all xyz against io (NVDA, SNDK, DRAM, CRCL, NBIS, AAPL, EWY and others) or
  xyz against para (UNITREE, IREN, AVGO and smaller), plus ANTH on io and para.
- **No lane: cross-dex funding carry.** xyz and io both use multiplier 0.5 and para 0.6 on the default formula, so
  their funding differs only through premiums, which flip. R443 found that such spreads on OKX against Hyperliquid
  flip within about a week and do not pay a round trip. Most io and para markets also trade under $1M a day.
