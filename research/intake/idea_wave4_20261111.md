# Idea wave 4: eight web-research lenses on France-reachable edges (intake, 2026-11-11)

The owner asked on 2026-11-11 for wide, batched web and deep searches for new ideas ("Tu veux pas batch des websearch
et deep search des trucs genre pleins"). Eight Haiku readers worked in parallel, one lens each. This page keeps:
- what they read, and what the author checked again;
- where each idea already sits in the lab;
- what was tested.

It is not evidence of a return. Searches hit rate limits in several lenses, so coverage is partial. Those queries are
listed under "not covered".

**Labels:**
- **[A]** checked again by the author: page or endpoint fetched and matched, or computed.
- **[V]** read on the fetched page by a helper.
- **[S]** search snippet only.
- **[U]** not found or not read.

## Tested in this wave
- **M512, USDT mints and BTC: R460 `experiments/usdtmint_v1`, NULL.**
  - Data:
    - 262 Tether `Issue` events, 2021-2026, from TronGrid and Blockscout [A];
    - the event topic keccak256("Issue(uint256)") was computed and checked against two known hashes [A];
    - six Ethereum events were re-checked on a second provider [A].
  - D1 (BTC, 24 h): +23 bp per trade against a +6 bp placebo; Holm p 0.40; holdout excess -13 bp; -3.4 bp at 2x
    cost. D2 (BTC, 4 h): Holm p 0.44.
  - Lens 8's claim (BTC +0.4-0.8% within 5-30 minutes of a mint, 2014-2021) [S] was not tested at that horizon. The
    1-hour cells (diagnostics) are near zero: +3.8 bp at 1 h.
- **M513, earnings on 24/7 stock perps: R461 `experiments/earnperp_v1`, NULL.**
  - Data: 1,735 8-K Item 2.02 filings timed by EDGAR acceptance (288 tickers) [A]; MEXC hourly stock perps, sample B
    fetched after the freeze [A].
  - After-close filings, first move followed to the next close: +22 bp (n 219, Holm p 1.0, MDE80 127 bp); to the next
    open + 30 min: +1 bp. Both lose money at 1x (30 bp).
  - Diagnostic only: before-open filings held to the close +80 bp in A and +70 bp in B (raw p 0.03 of 32 cells). A
    lead for a prospective lane on fresh filings, not evidence.

## Hyperliquid rules re-read in this wave (lens 5, [V])
- Funding cap 0.5% per hour, the same for every asset.
- F = average premium + clamp(interest - premium, -0.0005, 0.0005), with interest 0.00125% per hour.
- Premium is sampled every 5 seconds.
- Backstop liquidation below 2/3 of maintenance margin.

These are context for existing lanes, not edges.
