## Where each other idea sits
- **S&P 500 additions announced after the close** (lens 1).
  - Kappou (2017) via a CXO summary [V]: 228 additions, 2002-2013; an overnight gap of +3.2% and a post-crisis
    next-day open-to-close of -0.62%.
  - S&P DJI announces after the close by default [S].
  - On 24/7 perps the after-hours jump is reachable. Too few such events fall among the ~76 xyz US names a year.
  - New row M519 (BLOCKED): needs a time-stamped announcement archive [U].
- **Overnight secondary offerings** (lens 1).
  - Gustafson (JFQA 2018), abstract [V]: 75% of 2009-2014 offerings were overnight, and they show no post-issue
    reversal. Non-overnight issues show a 2.5% pre-issue fall that reverses within 7 days.
  - A "-9% next day" figure is practitioner-only [S].
  - New row M516 (QUEUED): EDGAR gives acceptance times (424B prospectuses, 8-K 8.01), and the lab holds MEXC hourly
    stock perps. It is testable like R461.
- **FDA decisions after the close** (lens 1; a CORE study, 2009-2015: 121 of 168 announcements after the close [S]).
  Very few xyz names (MRNA, TWST, XBI). Context only.
- **Fee levers** (lens 2; fee pages [V], undated):
