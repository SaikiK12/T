## Lens findings
### 1. CME gap: the regime ended, and the claim did not beat a random control
- **CME moved its crypto futures and options to 24/7 trading on 2026-05-29** [S, searched again by the author: several
  outlets agree, and CME's investor-relations page is listed; 2026-11-11].
  - The only scheduled stop is a Saturday 02:00-04:00 CT maintenance window.
  - The Sunday-reopen gap no longer exists as a venue event.
- **The only quantitative study found is a blog** (Tradewize, 427 weekends, December 2017 to May 2026, Binance
  spot) [V]. Fill rates of the gap level against a matched random level:
  - within 1 hour: 22% vs 30%;
  - within 1 day: 55% vs 65%;
  - within 1 week: 80% vs 83%.
  - The gap does no better than chance. No academic study of CME gap fill was found [U].
- **ETF era:** Kaiko (February 2024) puts the weekend share of BTC volume at 13% year to date, against 24% in 2018
  [V]. Baur et al. find weekend effects that are time-varying and not persistent [S].
- **Outcome:** CONTEXT. No lane; folklore whose regime has ended.

### 2. Pre-IPO and pre-listing perps: interesting mechanics, too few events
- **Mechanics:**
  - Hyperliquid hyperps [V hyperliquid.gitbook.io trading/hyperps]:
    - the mark uses a weighted median of CEX pre-launch perp prices, capped at 3x the 8-hour mark EMA and at 1.5x
      the median external perp;
    - the funding premium is 1% of the usual one;
    - the market "converts to a normal perp" shortly after spot trading starts on Binance, OKX or Bybit.
  - Binance pre-IPO perps [V press release, 2026-05-21]: settled if the IPO is cancelled.
  - Lighter lists pre-IPO markets (OpenAI, SpaceX, Anthropic, Quantinuum) [S]. Its docs say [A, `llms-full.txt`]:
    "If an IPO is delayed or withdrawn, the market keeps trading under the pre-IPO methodology."
- **Documented conversions** [V: CNBC/Talos, Alea Research]:

  | company | perp before conversion | first external price |
  |---|---|---|
  | SpaceX | about $162 | $150 open; $135 IPO price |
  | Cerebras | about $354.5 | $350 open |
  | Quantinuum | about $90 | $68 open |

  - One preprint (arXiv 2609.05433, abstract only [V]) argues that the perp premium is partly structural.
- **Incident:** a Ventuals SpaceX pre-IPO perp fell 45% on a bad off-chain feed on 2026-05-28, with 484
  liquidations [V The Block].
- **Power:** a premium fade needs about 70 conversions to detect a 10% mean premium at a 30% sd. About 3-5 exist.
- **Outcome:** mapped to M296 (pre-launch perp price as a forecast) as a note. Too few events to test; revisit when
  20 or more conversions exist.

### 3. Corporate actions and US holidays on 24/7 stock perps
- **Lighter does not adjust for splits** [A, `llms-full.txt`]: "Equity and ETF perp markets affected by a stock split
  or other corporate action are delisted and settled".
  - R465's and R466's universes therefore lack markets delisted that way (survivorship, stated in both PREREGs).
- **Other venues** [V venue help pages]:
  - **Kraken:** pays cash dividends from shorts to longs at the ex-date and scales positions on splits.
  - **Bitget:** pays dividends through funding at the prior extended-hours close.
  - **OKX X-Perps:** pay no dividends (2026-06-09) and may close positions on corporate actions.
  - **trade.xyz:** documents no split or dividend rule [U].
- **Holidays** [V NYSE calendar; trade.xyz Holiday Closures page]:
  - 2026-11-26 closed; 11-27 early close at 13:00;
  - 12-24 early close; 12-25 closed.
  - R462's rule already drops such weekends.
- **A US-closure gap fade** (the weekend fade over a weekday closure) gives about 9 full closures a year.
  - Queued as **M525**, UNTESTED.
  - Too rare to decide alone. It could join M515's forward record as a pooled observation.
- **Other ideas:**
  - Dividend-funding capture on Bitget: CONTEXT. The payment should be offset by the ex-date drop, and Bitget's
    access from France is unchecked.
  - Split ex-date flattening: CONTEXT, a risk control rather than an edge.

### 4. Costs: Lighter Standard stays the floor
- **Lighter Standard: 0 maker and 0 taker** [V docs.lighter.xyz trading-fees]. The helper read a 300 ms taker latency
  (maker 0 ms on the fetched page, 200 ms in snippets).
  - France is not in Lighter's restricted list [V lighter.xyz/terms, updated 2025-12-29].
- **Hyperliquid base tier: 1.5 bp maker / 4.5 bp taker** [V gitbook fees]. Maker rebates need a large share of
  14-day maker volume, out of reach at $1M a month.
- **trade.xyz:** 3.0 / 9.0 bp standard, with a growth table one tenth of that [V docs.trade.xyz fees]. R462's 0.9 bp
  growth taker fee matches it.
- **Others:**
  - Paradex retail UI is zero fee [S]; France unchecked.
  - Bybit EU has no retail derivatives in the EEA [S].
- **Collateral and points:**
  - No depositor yield on posted USDC was found on Lighter or Hyperliquid [U/S].
  - Points values are speculative; no reliable dollar value per $1M of volume was found [U].
- **Outcome:** a note on M475. Lighter Standard is the cheapest taker-taker and maker-maker venue reachable from
  France. Its latency suits hourly lanes, not tape scalping.

### 5. Papers: no demonstrated after-cost perp-DEX edge for a small public-data account
- **He, Manela, Ross and von Wachter,** arXiv 2212.06888 v7 (2026-09) [V abstract; S numbers]:
  - perp-spot arbitrage, with Sharpe 1.8 at high retail costs and up to 3.5 for zero-fee makers;
  - CEX data; the full text was not read.
  - The lab's carry lanes (R190 onward, R437) already measure this family.
- **Zhai,** arXiv 2608.04373 [V abstract]:
  - on a DEX with public counterparties, wallet identity predicts one-second returns (out-of-sample R² 12.3%);
  - no costs are reported, and the horizon is not tradable at our latency.
- **Garcia Seuma,** arXiv 2607.27070 and 2608.03616 [V abstracts]: cascade mechanics and precursors; no return test.
- **Barone and Lillo,** arXiv 2606.15715 [V abstract]: visible TWAPs on Hyperliquid cost less than hidden metaorders.
  This bears on cost, not alpha.
- **Data lead:** SSRN 6465720, "An Open Book: Level 4 Order Book Data from the Hyperliquid Exchange" [S, not read].
- **Outcome:** CONTEXT. None of these is a lane.

  - "The overnight trading session is provided by Blue Ocean ATS (BOATS)."

- **24-hour US stock trading** [S]: Nasdaq targets 23/5 from 2026-12-06; 24X's first stage has been live since

  - If US stocks trade through the night, the weekend is the only remaining closure; M515's window is unchanged.
