## M522: month-end rebalancing (Harvey, Mazzoleni and Melone)
- **Full text read [V]:** NBER w33554, version of 2026-01-14, at
  https://www.edhec.edu/sites/default/files/2026-03/Scientific%20paper.%20ssrn-5122748.pdf (SSRN 5122748).
- **Design [V]:**
  - a daily predictive regression, not a month-end event study;
  - signal: the equity weight of a simulated 60/40 ES/TY portfolio minus 60%, at day t's close;
  - Threshold signal: rebalance when the weight drifts beyond a band (mean over 0-2.5%);
  - Calendar signal: rebalance on the last business day, used on the last 5 trading days only;
  - target: the next day's close-to-close ES minus TY return.
- **Size [A, Table 1 re-read]:**
  - Threshold -0.4144 (se 0.1148, t -3.6);
  - Calendar × week4 -0.3029 (se 0.0808, t -3.7; corrected 2026-11-12 from the full text, the 0.0709 first noted
    is the Calendar term's se);
  - abstract: 17 bp of next-day equity return per signal sd.
- **Sample [A]:** 1997-2023, ending 2023-03-17 [V].
  - Footnote 32 [A]: "We have extended the sample through 2025 and find that the results become even stronger". So
    only 2026 is unseen by the authors.
  - 1961-1997 is insignificant (Table 4) [V].
- **Strategy [V]:** long ES and short TY by the signals, 10.2%/yr, Sharpe 1.11 gross (Table 6).
  - Costs get one sentence ("close to 1" net).
  - Parameters were chosen on the full sample.
  - Pension executives in the paper's roundtable know of the front-running.
- **Related [S]:** Etula et al., "Dash for Cash" (RFS 2020): pension selling peaks about T-3 to T-4 before month-end.
- **Where it stands:** a forward lane on trade.xyz's SP500 perp is the clean test, since the history is the authors'
  own sample.
  - It would short when the signal is high on the last five sessions, at about 30 signal days a year.
  - Equity-only, with no bond leg, its Sharpe would be lower than the paper's.
  - **Registered as R472** `experiments/rebalfront_v1` (frozen `78340957` before any SPY, IEF or TLT price was fetched).
    It rebuilds the paper's Table 6 rule on SPY/IEF closes. 2002-2023 checks the construction; 2023-03-20 ..
    2026-11-11 decides at trade.xyz's SP500 + TLT cost. xyz lists TLT too (growth mode), so both legs are reachable.

## M523: end-of-day momentum and dealer gamma
- **Baltussen, Da, Lammers and Martens, JFE 2021, full text [A]:** https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf
  - Table 6: the rest-of-day timing strategy on equity index futures earns 6.86%/yr gross with Sharpe 1.73 (1974-2020).
    That is about 2.7 bp a day.
  - Table 7, S&P 500 futures, 1996 to May 2020:
    - when the previous day's net gamma exposure is negative, the slope is 6.63 (t 4.78, R² 3.58%);
    - when it is positive, the slope is 0.82 (t 1.03, R² 0.05%).
  - They used SqueezeMetrics' GEX for the extension to 2020 [V].
- **Gao, Han, Li and Zhou (JFE 2018) [S]:** first half hour to last half hour on SPY 1993-2013, 6.3%/yr gross.
- **Barbon, Beckmeyer, Buraschi and Moerke [V, SFI WP 2021/14]:** end-of-day momentum in single stocks on negative
  gamma imbalance, ±2.87 bp.
- **0DTE era [S]:**
  - Adams et al. (SSRN 5641974) find dealers mostly long gamma and short-horizon reversal.
  - Dim, Eraker and Vilkov (SSRN 4692190) find no volatility amplification.
  - No study of 2021-2026 index last-half-hour momentum was found [U].
- **Our data [A, computed]:** SqueezeMetrics GEX (`data/squeezemetrics/DIX_20261106.csv`) is negative on only 18 of
  504 sessions since 2024-11-01.
- **Where it stands:** the effect lives on negative-gamma days, and those have almost vanished since late 2024.
  - Pooled, the historical 2.7 bp a day is below the 5.8 bp round trip.
  - Not registered.
  - Reopen it if negative-GEX sessions become common again; the MEXC SPX500 5-minute bars and xyz SP500 bars are held
    for that.
