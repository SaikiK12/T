## 1. trade.xyz oracle
- Roll window: "The roll period is the 5th-9th business day of the month" (https://docs.trade.xyz/llms-full.txt, Roll Schedules section). Scope: "markets that reference futures-based price feeds (e.g., COPPER, WTIOIL, BRENTOIL, NATGAS and DIESEL)".
- Weights (llms-full.txt, "from the 5th to the 10th business day"): BD5 100% front; BD6 80/20; BD7 60/40; BD8 40/60; BD9 20/80; BD10 100% next. That is five 20% cuts (BD6-BD10). A Hypercall post says "five scheduled 20% steps". Whether the first cut lands on BD5 or BD6 is inconsistent between sources (docs say 5th-9th; weights table starts at 80% on BD6). Not resolved.
- "Updated weightings take effect when the oracle switches back to external pricing at 6pm ET." Roll table headers use 5:30PM ET. The 17:30-18:00 window is consistent but not stated in those words.
- Hours: NATGAS and WTIOIL Sunday 6PM to Friday 5PM ET; BRENTOIL to Friday 6PM ET (https://docs.trade.xyz/perpetuals/specifications-and-schedules/specification-index.md).
- Designated months (llms-full.txt table, calendar month -> contract code referenced): WTIOIL and NATGAS Jan..Dec = G H J K M N Q U V X Z F (next calendar month, every month). BRENTOIL Jan..Dec = H J K M N Q U V X Z F G (two months ahead, every month). Examples: "WTIOIL is currently referencing the X6 contract and will roll into Z6"; NATGAS "K26 to M26" in April 2026 window; BRENTOIL "M6 to N6".
- NOT found: exchange or price-source publisher. The spec index says "Not specified" for all three. Whether NATGAS=NYMEX NG, WTIOIL=NYMEX CL, BRENTOIL=ICE Brent or CME BZ is not stated. Yahoo lists BZ on NYM. The Brent contract is unknown. I did not read the last ~40k characters of llms-full.txt (offset 200000 returned nothing relevant).
Verdict: partly verified (roll days, weights, months, hours verified; exchange/source/intraday timestamps not found).

## 5. Analyses
- Hypercall, "Settlement mechanics" https://insights.hypercall.xyz/oil-perps-settlement-2026-04-11 (read): "In listed futures the trader performs the roll. Here, the oracle does"; "the carry shows up as a persistent mark-to-oracle discount and a one-way funding bill from shorts to longs"; WTI paid "$4.9k per day per $1M" to longs, negative 24 of 24 hours; worst hourly about -878% annualized on April 8. Note the sign: in that backwardated roll, shorts PAID longs. It does not compare funding to roll yield.

Verdict: partly verified (one qualitative source; no quantitative funding-vs-roll study found).

## Recommendation
CME futures (MCL, MNG, or the standard contracts) rolled on xyz's own schedule track the oracle most closely, because xyz is itself a monthly front-to-next blend of every-month contracts (Brent two months ahead). Replicating the weights 100/80/.../0 over BD5-10 gives near-zero contract and roll-day mismatch. The WisdomTree ETCs follow Bloomberg "4W"/"Multi-Tenor" indices whose roll timing and tenors I could not verify, and BCOM's roll (reportedly BD6-10) and designated months may differ from xyz's. Expect a residual of different roll days (plus possibly different months, e.g. odd-month schedules for NG/CL if the index uses them) plus 0.49% TER and swap costs. For a paper lane, use the CME series (Yahoo live months, archived daily) as the reference hedge and treat any ETC only as a retail-implementable proxy, with an explicit tracking-error row. First settle: Brent source contract (ICE vs CME BZ), and the exact BD5 vs BD6 first weight cut.
