# What venue points programs paid, per dollar of volume: read by Claude, 2026-11-21 (Bet 3 of the breakthrough review)

- **Question.** Can a hedged book capture venue incentives (points, then tokens) for a return that matters?
  - The lab's rule: never count points at a hoped-for price. Measure realized value per unit of what earns points.
- **Sources.** Search summaries of news and data pages, listed below. No page was read in full. The numerators are
  well reported; the volume denominators are approximate. **Order of magnitude only.**

## What it means
- **Points pay per unit of volume:** about 1-3 bp of volume at launch prices on the two largest programs.
- **For a carry book they are noise.** $10k of capital turns over about $20k a month, worth a few dollars of points.
- **For a strategy that trades real volume, they are a rebate.** On a zero-fee venue, 1-3 bp of volume can turn a
  break-even make-and-hedge book positive.
  - So Bet 3 joins Bet 2: run the make-and-hedge book on a zero-fee venue with points, and count the points at zero in
    every decision. Report them as an option on top.
- **Kill.** Wash and self-trade filters remove volume that is not real risk-taking, so points never justify volume
  that loses before them.
