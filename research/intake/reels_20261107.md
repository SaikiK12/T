## Consequence implemented in the existing research work

These are two presentations of the same mechanism, not independent strategy
discoveries. Legitimate cancellations, executions, changing prices and outages
also move L2 depth; two snapshots cannot establish fraudulent intent or track
the same order. A candlestick cannot recover that missing order lifecycle.

Existing **M437** already targets Kraken order-uid/execution replay, and
**M439/M485** cover execution and leg risk. No duplicate method or broad chart
grid is opened. New large order-event acquisition remains subject to the
owner's existing download restriction; the reels do not authorize it.

Before its freeze, the inactive [R419 collector](../../experiments/eea_pair_capture_v1/PREREG.md)
was extended to reobserve the four named Coinbase/Kraken books after a fixed
**three-second wait following the first request group**, retaining actual
per-book request/receipt times. No same-order persistence or exact three-second
lag is asserted. Original cumulative byte/request caps are retained; the extra
observations may shorten the campaign. Only synthetic tests were run; no
recorder invocation or financial/depth statistic was calculated.

A subsequent separately frozen execution test must use information available
at the decision and the first eligible post-arrival observation, refuse stale
or insufficient depth, charge all costs at1x/2x/3x, retain failed/unfilled
attempts and measure conditional markout. Event-level fill validation still
needs M437's complete replay and initial state. Delayed L2 sweeps remain a
conditional fill model, not proof of actual fills. A depth-stability filter
would have to improve net outcomes on new dates; it is not established here.
