## Propfirm compounding: a hedged simulation, with a binding contract gap

[Quant / quant_models, published 2026-11-04](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfVJWnfMysj/).
The sampled frames show all seven numbered slides. The creator reports starting
capital **$11,400**, final simulated equity **$878,149**, and a January 2024–July
2026 backtest. The final disclosure says “résultats simulés, non réalisés”.
These are **the creator's claims**, not reproduced lab returns.

The mechanism is visible: buy in a propfirm account, sell in a personal account,
use the hedge to partly offset a failed challenge, then reinvest payouts into
additional paired units. A growing account count is not independent evidence:
shared positions, counterparties and payout rules can fail together.

Before any economic admission, account for real hedge cash/margin, unpaid
qualification phases, losses before a payout, profit splits, fees, reset charges,
allocation limits and actual enforceable payment terms. Reinvestment requires
cash that was paid; it cannot make an unqualified payout assumption reliable.

The exact cross-account mechanism is outside the project's existing
[never-do list](../../../docs/PROP_FIRMS.md). FTMO Futures also expressly prohibits
opposing positions across its products and other operators/platforms. The
separate CFD policy uses manipulative-purpose wording; these are not identical
policies, and no universal conclusion about every firm follows. No admitted
provider, reproducible code, contract terms or audited cash payouts were
established from the materials read. **Grade D: reported simulation, not validated net income.**
Keep M452 BLOCKED and M211 PARTIAL. R202 already flags optimistic wrapper models;
do not replay it or resurrect an unconfirmed input strategy. Ordinary permitted
reinvestment of a proven net book remains a different question.

## QQQ options flow: preserve the multi-leg and portfolio context

[Theta Warrior / thetawarriorpro, published 2026-11-07](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfdWYmkv6Cy/).
The reel labels the trade “$21,000,000 into QQQ” and “downside risk-reversal”.
Its screen shows **15,000 calls at strike 760** and **15,000 puts at strike 675**,
both labelled **11/30/26**, with two premium VALUE columns. The presenter
describes puts bought to open. These are screenshot claims; no independent
exchange tape, opening/closing audit or beneficial portfolio was obtained.

The amount displayed does not establish net new directional capital. The same
long-put/short-call structure against existing shares is a protective collar,
as the primary IBKR guide explains. Standard ETF contract deliverables, confirmed
by OCC, do not determine current option delta. Neither a large trade nor its
share-equivalent count supplies a directionally profitable copy signal.

The recovered picture at approximately **45 seconds** shows a **September 2026**
calendar claiming **76% win rate, 21 trades, 16 gains and 5 losses**. Retain this
claim rather than saying no results are displayed. It is not an audited account
statement: position sizes, all fills, costs, selection and portfolio returns are
unverified. **Grade D.**

Useful requirement for future flow work: keep complex-order linkage, trade
conditions, contemporaneous quotes, first-seen times and unknown position intent.
The current per-strike recorder is not that trade-level evidence. Link to M486;
do not retest R416's GEX null or treat this as a proven BTC signal. The
comment/follow-gated checklist is marketing, not a public verified track record.
