## Reel 1: "Top 3 things I'd do now with $500 in crypto"
**Grade D.** It is a sales funnel: the end card points to `skool.com/coinpicksgenesis`, and the caption offers "the whole system for a dollar".

**Claims, from the transcript:**
1. Spend $100 on Claude. Run 2,000 to 5,000 simulations of a Bitcoin direction bot until it is "above 50% accuracy". "Ours runs at about 58%."
2. Run a second bot that picks altcoins through fundamental analysis: "if it picks 10, six of them have to be good".
3. Run a third bot that manages the portfolio, with the three bots talking to each other.
4. Trade on a cheap layer 2 such as Base, start small, and scale up "as accuracy holds".
5. "In the beginning it'll be very profitable, but over time it will become less profitable". Then tweak it and back-test three to five bots at a time.

**Read against the lab:**
- **Hit rate is not return.** A 58% hit rate says nothing about net profit without the size of wins and losses and the costs. No cost, drawdown, out-of-sample period or track record is shown.
- **Picking the best of thousands of simulations is the trap the lab measured.** R263 ran 5,475 chart strategies:
  - its best single strategy had t = 3.11 in sample, but a reality-check p of 0.334;
  - the walk-forward selection earned +1.18 / +0.29 / −0.61 bp a day at 1x / 2x / 3x costs, with NW t = 0.96.
- **The decay the reel describes is what overfitting looks like.** R304's ML pick earned +58.9 bp a day (t = 4.21). On fresh validation in R332 it lost 16.5 bp a day: NULL.
- **Tweaking after decay adds more trials.** Re-tuning the bots when profits fade adds trials without fresh evidence.
- **The three-bot setup is not new to the queue.** It is the multi-agent pipeline already queued as M102 (BLOCKED: it needs a dated news and fundamentals archive). The direction bot falls under M042 and the R263 search.
- **Cheap layer-2 gas is not the whole cost.** DEX swap fees and slippage remain and dominate at a $400 stake. This was not measured here.
