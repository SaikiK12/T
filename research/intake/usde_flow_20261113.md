## Reading for R488
- **No published estimate** of USDe supply change against later funding was found. Lorig gives the mechanism as a
  model (impact lowers the basis); Ethena's docs confirm mints add perp shorts but do not name venues.
- **Sign.** The impact mechanism predicts that mints lower funding (rho < 0). The plan's gate tests rho > 0
  (inflows lead funding up, e.g. inflows chase high and persistent funding). R488 freezes the plan's one-sided test
  and reports the opposite-sign one-sided p and the two-sided p beside it, deciding nothing.
- **Venue gap.** Hyperliquid is not named as an Ethena venue. Its funding is linked to the hedged venues by
  arbitrage, so R488 reads a downstream market. A null on Hyperliquid does not rule out an effect on Binance, Bybit,
  OKX or Deribit funding (not keyless in this sandbox for Binance; not tested).
- **Supply is not hedge size.** Part of the backing is stable assets with no hedge, and the share moves over time;
  the supply change is a noisy proxy for the hedge change.
