# Intraday momentum + flow hybrids: bounded research review

**As of:** 2026-10-29. **Purpose:** identify a small number of new, cost-aware hypotheses; this is source synthesis and queue review only. No market statistics, data pulls, trials, or frozen-lane edits were made. Literature motivates hypotheses, not demonstrated net PnL.

## Recommendation

There are two bounded hypotheses below. The quarter-hour flow result aligns unusually well with a 4–12-hour holding period, but the paper’s own direct 10-second forecast earns only about 0.51 bp gross per trade versus a 5 bp Binance taker fee on one side. Treat flow as a pre-registered confirmation or state variable for a multi-hour trade, never as standalone taker alpha. A BTC-only rule is narrower than the R371 cross-sectional overlay, but its source-faithful 10-second imbalance still needs trade-level data; one-minute bars can support only a separately specified bar-based flow variant. Neither hypothesis should alter R371, R355, R364, R304, or R305.

## What the primary studies support

Kim and Hansen use Binance USDT-perpetual aggregate trades for BTC, ETH, XRP, SOL, DOGE, and ADA (2021-01 to 2024-10). Their signed imbalance for a boundary interval is buyer-taker minus seller-taker volume divided by total volume. At the first 10 seconds after a quarter-hour boundary, opening imbalance is positively associated with subsequent cumulative returns at 4–12 hours; the paper says that medium-horizon relation is concentrated at the quarter-hour grid. This is a predictive association, not an identified causal price impact and not a cost-tested strategy. In a separate rolling monthly walk-forward forecast of the next boundary’s first 10-second return, the combined lag/technical-indicator forecast averages 0.510 bp gross per trade and 56.6% hit rate; the paper compares that with a 5 bp standard-tier Binance taker fee per side and concludes the forecast is better viewed as an execution/liquidity-provision input. [Kim & Hansen, *The Quarter-Hour Effect* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09426)

Shen, Urquhart, and Wang report that BTC’s first half-hour predicts its last half-hour, and that predictability is strongest in the first trading sessions with the highest volume or volatility. Their proposed trading-time proxy is volume because BTC has no conventional open or close. [Shen, Urquhart & Wang, *Bitcoin Intraday Time Series Momentum* (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1111/fire.12290)

Gao, Han, Li, and Zhou find the first half-hour after the previous close predicts the final half-hour in S&P 500 ETFs; the pattern strengthens on high-volume, high-volatility, recession, and major-news days. That is equity evidence. Applying its cash-session clock to a continuously traded BTC perp is an inference, not a crypto result. [Gao et al., *Market Intraday Momentum* (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2018.05.009)

## Candidate 1 — R371-style cross-sectional leaders, QH-flow confirmation

**Question.** Does a quarter-hour flow sign confirm which R371 leaders can be held through the same 9–10-hour US-open-to-midnight window, after actual MEXC costs?

**Frozen rule to consider in a separate sibling lane.** Keep R371’s 10:00 New York decision, top-50 universe, and top-three/bottom-three ranking by return since 00:00 UTC. At decision time `D`, define for each selected coin the 15-minute realized volatility `RV15 = sqrt(sum of squared one-minute log returns in [D−15m,D))` and quote turnover `QV15 = sum quote volume over [D−15m,D)`. Using only observations available by `D`, set `active_i=1` iff both `RV15` and `QV15` exceed that coin’s trailing 30-day median for the same 15-minute clock window. Define the first-10-second quarter-hour imbalance

`OI_i(D) = (Σ buyer-taker USDT notional − Σ seller-taker USDT notional) / Σ total USDT notional`, over `[D,D+10s)`.

Use Binance USD-M `aggTrade` (`isBuyerMaker=false` means buyer taker) and point-in-time receipt time. At `D+10s`, a long candidate is confirmed only if `OI_i>0` and `active_i=1`; a short only if `OI_i<0` and `active_i=1`. Enter confirmed positions at R371’s existing MEXC one-minute open at or after `D+3m`; exit at its existing midnight-plus-three-minute open. Preserve unit gross on each side by equally weighting confirmed names within each side only when at least two of the original three names on **each** side pass; otherwise that day is flat. Rejected legs remain cash and are never replaced by other ranks. This alignment gives the flow signal time to form before the already-specified fill and targets the paper’s 4–12-hour association. The two-confirmed-per-side rule, activity medians, timestamps, and missing-trade behavior must all be frozen before a new sample.

**Instrument, inputs, and coverage.** MEXC-listed crypto perps with the Binance-to-MEXC mapping already used by R371; Binance aggregate trades and 1-minute bars; MEXC minute opens, measured spread, listed taker fee, and funding. Current R371 records a top-50 signal but the point-in-time `data/tape` recorder covers only 12 named coins, starting 2026-10-21; historical `aggTrades` are not in the repo. It therefore cannot honestly run this full-top-50 flow filter today. Either establish complete on-time flow capture for every possible R371 pick before opening the new lane, or define a different fixed, tape-covered universe in a separate preregistration. A partial flow feed must not silently convert missing trades into zero imbalance.

**Costs and hurdle.** Score at 1x/2x/3x with actual MEXC taker fees, entry/exit half-spreads, funding, missed/late fills, and a conservative slippage allowance fixed before the sample. R371’s one plumbing day showed about 21 bp aggregate MEXC book cost, with costs varying by contract; its API-cost comparison is 34 bp per day. The QH paper’s 0.51 bp gross 10-second forecast is far below even one standard-tier taker fee, so this hypothesis must earn its return from the later multi-hour move. Primary claim should be positive net 1x and incremental net benefit over the unmodified R371 rule on identical scored days and account denominator; 2x positive and a positive mean excluding the ten best days are necessary robustness checks, not substitutes for the paired increment.

**Sample and power.** Only days after a separate preregistered freeze count. Use a 500-scored-NYSE-day terminal holdout as the planning floor, with a single futility look only if its boundary and power are calibrated before first ticket; record all eligible and flat days. Before freeze, calculate the minimum detectable paired net improvement using training-only or synthetic dependence-preserving simulations and the measured cost distribution. If the required effect is larger than a plausible multi-hour edge or the activity filter leaves too few observations, do not start. Do not reuse R300’s 2021–2026 windows, R371 tickets, or the QH paper’s sample as validation.

**Overlap and null checks.** R300/M318 already tested the unfiltered 00:00-to-US10 cross-sectional momentum family and was NULL; R371 is the owned prospective replication and must stay unchanged. M264 is queued for 1–10-second tape scalps; R349/M426 tested five-minute flow extremes (fade survived historically on zero-fee Lighter but weakened recently and failed at MEXC costs); R349/M306 absorption/exhaustion is NULL; R291/M085 VPIN is NULL. Those are related flow families but not the same 4–12-hour, quarter-hour-conditioned cross-sectional claim. Count all these family choices in the new lane’s disclosure and use only its prospective days. M319’s 08:00–16:00 UTC relative-strength hold is separately queued and not a substitute control.

## Candidate 2 — BTC cash-session half-hour momentum with QH confirmation

**Question.** Does first-half-hour BTC perp momentum predict a later US-session move only when 09:30 boundary flow agrees and opening activity is high?

**One-rule design.** On NYSE sessions only, let `D=09:30 America/New_York`. Over `[D,D+10s)`, compute `OI_BTC(D)` using the same USDT-notional imbalance formula above. At `D+30m`, compute `r30 = ln(mid(D+30m)/mid(D))`, `RV30 = sqrt(sum squared one-minute log returns over [D,D+30m))`, and `QV30 = total USDT quote volume in that interval`. Set the activity condition only if **both** `RV30` and `QV30` exceed BTC’s trailing 30-day median for the same NYSE opening half-hour, calculated from data available by `D+30m`. Trade long iff `r30>0`, `OI_BTC(D)>0`, and the condition is true; trade short iff both signs are negative and the condition is true; otherwise hold cash. Enter at the next MEXC BTC one-minute open after `D+30m`; exit at the 15:30 New York one-minute open. This uses one instrument, a single open clock, no thresholds fitted to returns, and a roughly 5.5-hour holding period inside the QH paper’s reported 4–12-hour association. The half-hour-to-last-half-hour mechanism comes from the BTC and equity studies; this exact combined crypto rule has not been established in either paper.

**Instrument, inputs, and coverage.** BTCUSDT perpetual signal on Binance; MEXC BTC perp execution with one-minute opens, actual spread, fee, and funding. Current raw trade tape supports BTC and the `data/tape` 12-coin list only from 2026-10-21, so the source-faithful 10-second imbalance is forward-only unless a separate allowed historical aggTrades source is approved. A 2026-10-29 room update reports that Claude's main runtime has BTC/ETH/SOL 1-minute bars with `taker_buy_volume` back to 2020. Those bars can support a new 15-minute bar-based flow rule, but cannot reconstruct the paper's first-10-second trade imbalance. All 2021+ history is already spent; the 2020 sample is reserved until the batch-2 read window (on or after 2026-11-17) and no batch-2 preregistration exists. The current Codex checkout does not have those raw `data/` files. Check same-day receipt before any prospective scoring; late arrivals or gaps are ineligible, never backfilled into a “live” result.

**Costs and hurdle.** One daily round trip: actual MEXC contract taker fee on both fills, measured half-spread at each fill, funding over the actual holding interval, and fixed slippage stress. Report 1x/2x/3x. Require the hybrid to be net-positive at 1x and positive at 2x, and to beat the preregistered same-clock `sign(r30)` momentum-only parent on paired net returns; otherwise a high-activity filter can appear helpful merely by holding cash. Use cash as the profitability benchmark. No paper result is inferred from the published “economic gains” language because the studies use different markets, periods, and execution assumptions.

**Sample and power.** Freeze before first ticket and score 500 future NYSE sessions (or a longer fixed duration if pre-freeze power against measured costs requires it); zero-trade days remain zero. One preregistered futility look may be used only with boundaries calibrated on synthetic or training-only serially dependent returns. The flow feed starts in October 2026, so 500 scored sessions imply roughly two calendar years of live capture. This is slow but gives a genuine, contiguous holdout. Report MDE, eligible-day fraction, and latency/gap attrition before opening the lane; if the expected net edge is below the MDE, keep it as a descriptive paper monitor rather than a confirmatory trial.

**Overlap and null checks.** M301/R289 tested a US-cash-open 5–30-minute shock fade and was NULL; the proposed direction and 5.5-hour horizon differ, but it shares the same US-open clock and must be included in the research family. R203’s first-hour/last-hour test and R290’s session-anchor test are also relevant nulls. R371 is an overlapping prospective US10 clock exposure, although its cross-sectional return signal and midnight exit differ; do not pool its outcomes or claim independence if both use the same future dates. No reuse of its tickets or any pre-freeze historical returns.

## Which work has higher information value now?

**Do not blend QH flow into M042/R304 yet.** R304’s clean GBM reported +58.9 bp/day net 1x (t=4.21), but its 2022–2026 discovery/validation windows were already read in R299/R304; R304 itself calls the result gate 0 and not a candidate. R305 found the source unexplained, the long leg carries most of the alpha (+45.9 bp/day versus equal weight, t=8.81), the short leg is weak (+14.6, t=1.43), and 15.5% of validation weeks contained a short name up more than 100% (maximum +1,850%). R304 already includes daily taker/flow, volume, volatility, funding, and positioning features. An intraday flow add-on would be a new fitted interaction on spent windows and would obscure the registered R305 follow-ups: MEXC forward paper for the frozen GBM, long-leg/equal-weight hedge, and protected-short variant. Preserve that order.

The reserved 2020 rows could provide a historical regime check, but they contain no M042 metrics and no historical aggTrades for the source-faithful 10-second QH signal. The newly reported one-minute bars could support a different 15-minute flow adaptation, but that is a changed hypothesis and still needs its own frozen spec; no batch-2 preregistration exists, and the reserved read window starts no earlier than 2026-11-17. Scoring rows without M042 features changes the model; training on later years and calling 2020 an untouched forward holdout also reverses time. A MEXC-only historical batch uses currently listed-contract history and does not by itself solve delisted-contract coverage or demonstrate fills. Thus 2020/MEXC-only work can answer a carefully frozen robustness/data-coverage question, but it is lower expected information for executable PnL than the precommitted R305-style fresh MEXC forward variants.

R355 and R364 are also forward-only and presently show 0/80 scored on-time hops each in the panel. They are sparse settlement-event studies, so this early zero is “unassessed,” not a null. The panel also reports severe processing lag (218 on-time hours delayed; median 347.4 minutes). That makes same-day timestamps and receipts the binding readiness test for any new intraday strategy. First confirm that event and market data reach scoring on time; otherwise a daily hybrid cannot generate more reliable evidence merely by issuing more tickets. If freshness recovers, Candidate 2 is the narrowest new independent test; Candidate 1 should wait for complete top-50 flow coverage. R304’s registered MEXC variants and existing R355/R364 should continue unchanged.
