## Candidate identifiers and deduplication decision

- **Keep R285/M281 intact.** It is the existing prospective cross-family experiment. Wait for its preregistered read; do not alter weights, sleeve definitions, or its writer's journal.
- **For the ML family, continue under M042's already stated next steps only.** R304/R305 specify forward paper for the frozen pick; two variants that still need their own frozen lanes (the long leg against an equal-weight-universe hedge, and a protective cover on each short); the missing-metrics audit; and a batch-2 treatment for absent 2020 metrics. Each new test needs its own lane ID and untouched dates. The exact squeeze-cap rule, instrument, fill/stop mechanics, cost model, leverage, and comparison must be fixed before any read. R298 is context for stop behavior, not a substitute result.
- **Do not run M225 on its current specification as a “new” signal.** It reuses R238's 2021–23 discovery window. If retained at all, change its sample to genuinely unseen data before preregistration and account for all trials.
- **Treat M319 as a separate, already queued momentum candidate**, only on reserved 2020 data or prospective paper as its note requires. R371 already owns its different US-open forward question.
