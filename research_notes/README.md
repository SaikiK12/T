# Supporting research notes

[Repository home](../README.md) · [Narrative reports](../reports/README.md)

Subfolders preserve the supporting notes for named reports. The
`Project direction from quant evidence/` folder contains checks of source
literature, registry/trial accounting, execution economics and evidence integrity
for the [corresponding report](../reports/Project%20direction%20from%20quant%20evidence.md).

These are dated review inputs. Their counts and judgments belong to their
original scope; use the [registry](../registry/README.md) and native experiment
evidence for current decisions. `python -m lab find TEXT` can locate their
headings and paths without opening result payloads or journals.
